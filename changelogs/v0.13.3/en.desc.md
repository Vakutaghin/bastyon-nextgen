# v0.13.3 — Your own profile without needless buttons, only a real cover

Your own page no longer has buttons that led to a chat or a subscription with yourself, and the cover strip at the top of a profile appears only if the author has set one. If your computer has 0.9.4 or newer, the “Update” button installs this version (except the .deb package). On the phone, “Update” works from 0.12.0 too; from an earlier version, install this one by hand.

## Profile

- **You can’t write to yourself.** Your own page has no **“Start chat”** button, and your own posts have no chat button in the header.
- **No “Paid subscription” button on your own page.** You can’t subscribe to yourself; your price and your subscribers are still in the account menu: **“Paid subscriptions”**, the **“My subscribers”** tab.
- **Only the author’s own cover.** If the author has not set a cover, there is no strip at the top of the profile: the page starts right with the avatar and name. It used to show an empty placeholder or a blurred avatar. If the cover fails to load, there is no strip either.
- **Your own profile no longer gets stuck on “Registration in progress”.** If you opened it right after the app started, while the account data was still loading, that notice stayed until a reload. Now the profile finishes loading on its own.
