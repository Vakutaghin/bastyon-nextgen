# v0.21.0 — Search and privacy in chats

Search chats and people right in the messenger, “online”, “typing” and “recording a voice message” settings, sections in the notifications window. If your computer has 0.9.4 or newer, the “Update” button installs this version (except the .deb package). On the phone, “Update” works from 0.12.0 too; from an earlier version, install this one by hand.

## Chats

- **Search.** The magnifier in the messenger header, next to the cross, opens a field above the list. Up to five of your chats, groups and channels with that name show up at once, and below them up to fifteen people on Bastyon with that name. Tap a person to open your conversation with them, existing or new. **Enter** opens the first result, **Esc** closes the search.
- **Privacy.** “Settings” → “Chats” → **“Privacy”**: whether to show that you are online, typing or recording a voice message. The setting is shared by all your devices.
- **“Online”.** Off by default, as before. Turn it on, and people on Bastyon NextGen see “online” or “last seen …” in the chat header, and you see theirs if they share it too.
- **“Recording a voice message…”.** While you hold the microphone button, the other person sees that you are recording.
- **“Typing…”** now really shows up for the other person: before, it never appeared.

## Notifications

- **Sections.** Besides “All” and “Comments” there are **“Ratings”**, **“Followers”**, **“PKOIN”** (transfers, donations, paid subscriptions, network rewards) and **“Posts”** (reposts, boosts, new posts of authors with the bell on). Only sections that have something are shown, each with the number of new ones.
