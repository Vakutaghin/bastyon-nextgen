# v0.36.0 — pinned chats, monetization for authors

Chats with the people you write to most can now be pinned to the top of the list. The wallet has a page about how authors earn PKOIN from advertisers. If your computer has 0.9.4 or newer, the “Update” button installs this version (except the .deb package). On the phone, “Update” works from 0.12.0 too; from an earlier version, install this one by hand.

## Pinned chats

- **“Pin” in the chat menu.** The “⋯” menu of a chat in the list has “Pin” and “Unpin”. A pinned chat stays at the top of the list, above the newest chats and channels, however many new messages arrive in others. The most recently pinned one is on top.
- **A pin instead of the counter.** While a pinned chat has nothing new, a gray pin sits on its right. When a message arrives, the unread counter takes its place as usual.
- **Pins are the same on all your devices** with the new app: they are stored on the chat server, and the people you chat with do not see them. The old Bastyon does not show pins.
- **“Send to chat”** puts pinned chats first too.
- Channels, invitations and mesh chats cannot be pinned. If the server does not accept a pin, the chat goes back to its place and the app says so.

## Monetization for authors

- **The “Monetization for authors” page:** a diagram of how advertisers’ money reaches authors — from promoted posts through readers’ ratings and comments to payouts. Below it: who takes part, when payouts arrive, how to cash out PKOIN and how to earn more. The page opens without signing in.
- **Where to find it:** in the wallet “Earnings” tab, at bastyon.com/monetization (which used to open a profile with that name) and in Help — the new “Monetization for authors” article in the “Wallet and PKOIN” book.
- Payouts arrive as ordinary transfers and are counted in “Received as transfers and tips”.
