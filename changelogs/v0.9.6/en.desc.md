# v0.9.6 — Services in plain sight and app details for support in one tap

Mini-apps are now called “Services” and are easy to find: a button in the header and a shelf of popular services in the feed. The chat with support has a button that sends app details to look into a bug. Voice input on Windows and a few bugs in chats and the header are fixed. If your computer has 0.9.4 or 0.9.5, the “Update” button installs this version (except the .deb package).

## Services

- **New name and a header button.** The “Mini-apps” section is renamed “Services”, and on a computer a new button in the header opens it. On a phone the section is in the bottom bar.
- **A shelf in the feed.** The “Feed” shows popular services by user ratings. You can hide the shelf with the cross and bring it back on the “Services” page.
- **Sections instead of tags.** The “Services” page has sections: music, video, games, learning, finance and more. Built-in services now have descriptions.

## Chats

- **App details for support.** In the chat with support, an “App details” button is pinned above the conversation. It collects the version, system, settings and recent errors, and shows everything before sending. Keys, the recovery phrase and messages are not included. In the chat the details look like a small box with a copy button.
- **A name instead of “#…”.** In the chat list, a private chat sometimes showed a string like “#ac4fe523…”. Now it shows the person’s name or, while the profile loads, their address.

## Fixes

- **Voice input on Windows.** A brief glitch in the microphone sound stopped dictation with an error. Now recording goes on. If Windows blocks microphone access, the app says so and offers to open the right settings page.
- **The header after a service.** After coming back from an open service, the buttons and the account on the right of the header disappeared. Fixed.
- **The “Edit” button.** The network accepts edits to posts and comments only during the first 30 days. The button now disappears after 28 days instead of staying forever.
- **The “For developers: open your own app” window** is styled again instead of showing bare fields.
