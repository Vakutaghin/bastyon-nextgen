# Account badges

What the “Verified”, “Moderator”, “Shark” and “Established” badges mean and what they are given for.

## Where you see them

- **In a profile**, under the name: coloured badges. Tap a badge to read what it means.
- **On an avatar** — in the feed, comments, search, follower lists, notifications and chats — a mark in the top left corner: a blue check is “Verified”, a gold crown is “Moderator”, a purple flag is “Shark”. There is one mark: if several apply, you see the senior one, in this order. Hover over the mark to see its name.

“Established” has no mark on the avatar; the badge is only in the profile.

Who gives the badges: “Verified” — the Bastyon team, “Moderator” and “Shark” — the network itself, “Established” — the app, by reputation. You can’t ask for a badge.

## Verified

An account on the list of verified accounts kept by the Bastyon team. The list is built into the app and is the same as in the previous Bastyon app.

## Moderator

An experienced member whom the network calls to a jury when an account collects many reports — see [Moderation and rules](moderation.md). The network gives the badge itself if:

- the account is at least 90 days old;
- at least 100 different people have rated its posts and comments;
- at least 25 of them rated its comments.

The network recounts badges itself and removes them when the conditions stop being met, for example when the account is deleted.

## Shark

The most experienced member: the same conditions as for a moderator, but the account has to be at least a year old. The badge is not just for show:

- only sharks’ ratings move authors’ [reputation](reputation.md); other people’s ratings only go into the post’s rating;
- only sharks can [report](reports.md) posts, comments and accounts.

## Established

An account with a [reputation](reputation.md) of 100 or more. The app gives this badge, not the network. With such a reputation the account stops being a trial one (see [Account status and limits](limits.md)) and can give a post 1–3 stars.
