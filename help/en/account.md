# Account and security

Your account is a set of keys, not a login and password. How to keep them, protect them on your device and not lose them.

## The account is its keys

Bastyon has no server that keeps your password and can reset it. From the [recovery phrase](glossary.md#recovery-phrase) — 12 words — a private key is derived, and from the key the account's [address](glossary.md#address). Every action you take on the network is signed with the key, and your coins sit on the address.

The main consequence: whoever knows the phrase controls the account and the coins. Lose the phrase and nobody can give it back. If someone else learns it, there is no way to take the access away from them.

## Three rules

1. **Write the phrase down offline** — on paper or in a password manager, not as a screenshot and not in cloud notes.
2. **Never show it to anyone.** Support, moderators and mini apps will never ask for the phrase. Whoever asks is a scammer.
3. **Check your copy.** The app can verify that the words you wrote down are correct — better to find a mistake now than when your phone is lost.
