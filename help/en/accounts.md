# Several accounts and signing out

How to keep several accounts in the app, switch between them and sign out without losing anything.

## Adding an account

The account menu → **“Switch account”** → **“+ Add account”**. Then as usual: sign in with another account's phrase or key, or sign up a new one — see [Signing in](sign-in.md) and [Signing up](create-account.md). The previous account stays on the device.

## Switching

The **“Switch account”** window lists all accounts saved on this device; the current one is marked **“Current”**. Click another one to switch: you do not need to enter the phrase again.

Each account on the device has its own:

- favorites, post and comment drafts, search history;
- chats and message encryption keys;
- [my files](my-files.md) in IPFS.

Shared by all accounts are the language, the theme and the rest of the app settings.

## Signing out

**“Sign out”** in the account menu (or the sign-out icon next to an account in the **“Switch account”** window) removes that account from the device:

- its keys and the saved phrase;
- its favorites, drafts, search history and cached conversations;
- its list of IPFS files — the device stops sharing them.

The account itself does not go anywhere: posts, subscriptions, coins and chats stay on the network. You can come back at any time — but only with the recovery phrase or the key. That is why the app warns you that the action cannot be undone.

> [!WARNING]
> Before signing out, check that your phrase is written down — see [Recovery phrase and private key](recovery-phrase.md). Without it, signing out means losing the account.

If there are other accounts on the device, the app switches to the next one.

## Deleting an account for good

This app cannot delete an account from the network yet. The previous Bastyon app can do it in its settings. Others see a deleted account labeled "Account deleted".
