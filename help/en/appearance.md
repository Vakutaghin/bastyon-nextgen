# Language and appearance

How to change the interface and feed language, the theme, the scale and animations.

## Language

Change the language in the header — with the flag button next to the logo — or in **“Settings”** → **“General”** → **“Language”**. On a phone and in a narrow window the header has no flag; the language is in the settings only. There are nine languages: Russian, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Serbian, Korean and Chinese.

The feed has its own language: the app passes it to the node, which picks posts, tags and recommendations in that language. Changing the interface language switches the feed to it too. To read the feed in another language without changing the interface, use the **“Feed language”** field under the **“Best first”** switch in the left panel; on a phone it is in the side menu. See [Feed](feed.md).

The help exists in Russian and English. With any other interface language it opens in English.

## Theme

**“Settings”** → **“General”** → **“Theme”**:

- **“Auto”** — follows the device’s system: if the dark theme is on there, it is dark here too;
- **“Light”** or **“Dark”** — always that one.

To switch quickly, use the sun or moon button in the header.

## Page width

On a wide screen the page can be shown in two ways. **“Settings”** → **“General”** → **“Page width”**:

- **“Limited”** — the header, feed and pages form a column up to 1280 pixels in the middle of the screen, with empty space at the sides;
- **“Full width”** — the whole window: the side panels move to the edges, the feed, profile posts and search take all the space between them, and the **“Video”** and **“Media studio”** sections fit more clips in a row.

Full width is the default. To switch quickly, use the arrows button in the header, next to the theme. The **“Button in the header”** switch, also in **“General”**, or the **“Page width”** item in **“Header and panels”** hides it. The button shows when the window is wider than 1200 pixels: in a window narrower than the column there is nothing to switch. Phones and tablets have no such setting — the page always takes the whole screen there.

## Scale

In the desktop app: **“Settings”** → **“System”** → **“Interface scale”** — from 80 to 150%. Ctrl and + or − change the same scale by the same steps, and Ctrl+0 returns to 100% (Cmd on a Mac). A pinch on the touchpad does not change the scale. In a browser, the browser itself handles the scale.

## Animations

**“Settings”** → **“System”** → **“Animations”** — smooth transitions in the interface. Turn them off if the app feels sluggish or the animations bother you.
