# The Audio section

Bastyon music, audiobooks and podcasts: categories, My music, playlists and a player that keeps playing while you move around the app.

The section opens with **“Audio”** in the left panel, on a phone — in the side menu. Tracks come as a list: cover, title, author and length. The buttons on the right above the feed switch the view: **“Track list”** or **“Posts”** — whole posts, as in the main feed, with the player, text and comments. The view is remembered on this device.

## Tabs and categories

- **“Home”** — tracks from the whole network in the feed language. The **“All”**, **“Music”**, **“Audiobooks”** and **“Podcasts and talks”** categories pick them by the post's tags — “music”, “audiobook”, “podcast” and similar: audio on the network has no genre field. A track without such tags shows only under All.
- **“Subscriptions”** — tracks by the authors you follow.
- **“My music”** — your private list — see [Private lists](media-library.md).
- **“Playlists”** — your albums and playlists, public and private — see [Collections and playlists](collections.md).

The artist is the author of the post: their tracks, albums and playlists are on the **“Audio”** tab of their profile.

## The mini player

Tap a track — it plays in the mini player, followed by the rest of the list in order. On a computer the player sits in the lower left corner, on a phone it is a bar above the bottom panel, and it does not stop while you move around the app.

- The buttons are the previous track, pause, next, the queue and closing the player. Previous in the middle of a track goes back to its start.
- Tap the title or the cover to open the track's post.
- The queue holds the whole list: tap a track to jump to it; there are also **“Shuffle”** and repeat — off, the whole list or one track.
- You can control the player from the keyboard, headphones and the lock screen.
- Recordings longer than ten minutes — audiobooks, talks — continue where you stopped, even after a restart. The position is kept on this device.

The mini player and the player in a post never play at the same time: start one and the other pauses.

## The track menu

The three-dot button on a track: **“Play next”**, **“Add to queue”**, **“Add to My music”** and **“Add to…”** — to a private or public playlist.

## Search

The node does not include audio in its search, so the search field looks for tracks on the PeerTube servers recordings are uploaded to now, by title and description. Recent tracks are found, recordings on older servers are not. You can find those by tags — tap a tag under a post — or on the author's page.
