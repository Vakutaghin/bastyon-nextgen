# Channels

You can read any author’s posts in the messenger, next to your chats: the list shows what they have published since, and the posts themselves run as a feed, like a conversation.

## Adding a channel

On the author’s profile press **“Read in Messenger”**. You don’t need to subscribe, and there is no transaction: the channel appears at once. The button turns into **“Open in Messenger”**, and a button with a crossed-out megaphone appears next to it — **“Stop reading in Messenger”**.

On a phone these buttons are at the top of the profile, under the name, together with **“Subscribe”** and the chat button.

A channel and a subscription are independent. A subscription is the “Subscriptions” feed and, with the bell, notifications about new posts, see [Subscriptions](subscriptions.md). A channel is a way to read the author in the messenger.

## A channel in the chat list

A channel can’t be mistaken for a person or a group: it has the author’s square avatar with a megaphone in the corner and a violet megaphone before the name. Under the name — the kind of the last post (“Photo”, “Video”, “Article”, “Repost”) and the start of its text.

The number of new posts is in a violet bubble. It is not added to the message count: channels make no sound and send no notifications.

Above the list there are **“All”**, **“Personal”**, **“Groups”** and **“Channels”** switches. The last three go in any combination: for example, **“Groups”** and **“Channels”** together. The choice is remembered and shared by the messenger window and the full-screen messenger. If another section has something new, its switch shows a number.

The three-dot button on a channel:

- **“Open profile”** of the author;
- **“Mark as read”** — when there are new posts;
- **“Stop reading in Messenger”** — the channel leaves the list. You can bring it back by clicking the notification that appears, or again from the profile.

## The channel feed

Posts run like messages: older above, newer below, with a date over each day. If something new has come out since last time, the channel opens at the **“New posts”** line. Scroll up to load earlier ones.

Press a post to open it in full: with ratings, comments and the “Repost” and “Share” buttons. Long text is cut in the feed, followed by **“Read more”**.

Pictures sit in a grid, as in the feed; press a picture to look at it closely. A video has a cover with a ▶ sign: press it, and the post opens with the video already playing. YouTube and Vimeo videos linked in a post open the same way. With Tor on there are no covers: a picture from another server would bypass Tor.

On a phone posts take the full width of the screen, without bubbles, and pictures and videos run edge to edge.

At the bottom, instead of the message field: **“Subscribe”**, or **“Subscribed”** if you already are, and **“Message the author”**, which opens your private chat with them. While the subscription waits to be written to the blockchain, a spinner turns next to it. To unsubscribe, use the author’s profile.

Subscriber-only and paid posts look the same as in the feed: those closed to you show a lock.

## On all your devices

The list of channels and what you have read in them are kept on the chat server with your account. A channel added on your phone appears on your computer too, and what you read on one device isn’t new on another. The previous Bastyon app doesn’t show these channels.

The app checks for new posts while the messenger is open, every few minutes.
