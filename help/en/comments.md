# Comments

How to comment and reply, mention people, edit and delete comments, and rate other people’s comments.

## Where comments are

The discussion is under every post. In the feed you see the latest comment, and the buttons under it expand the rest: “Show 15 more” shows the next fifteen, **“Show all”** shows them all at once. **“Collapse”** hides the list again. On the [post page](post-card.md#the-post-page) the whole discussion is open.

## Writing a comment

1. Click the field at the bottom of the discussion and write your text. The smiley button is **“Emoji”**.
2. Click **“Send”** — the arrow button — or press Ctrl+Enter, on a Mac Cmd+Enter.

A comment can be up to 915 characters long. When fewer than 500 are left, a counter appears under the field; if the text is longer than allowed, the counter turns red and the comment cannot be sent.

An unfinished comment on a post is not lost: come back to the post and the text will be in the field. The draft is kept on this device, separately for each account. Unfinished replies are not saved.

Images in comments written in the previous Bastyon app are shown, but you cannot attach an image to a comment in this app.

## Replying

- **“Reply”** under a comment opens a reply field right below it.
- **“Reply to author”** does the same, but the reply already starts with the comment author’s name.

A discussion has two levels: comments on the post and replies to them. A reply to a reply goes into the same thread. The “Replies” link with a number under a comment expands the thread, **“Collapse replies”** collapses it.

## Mentioning someone

Type `@` and a list of people already taking part in the discussion appears. Keep typing the name to narrow the list, then pick a person with the arrow keys and Enter or with the mouse. In the published comment the mention becomes a link to the profile.

## Until the network confirms

Your comment appears right away, marked **“Pending”** — **“Waiting for network confirmation”**. Once the network confirms it, usually within a minute, the mark disappears. While it is there, the comment cannot be edited, deleted or rated.

If the network rejects the comment, the app shows the reason and puts the text back in the field: fix it and send it again. What the reasons mean is explained in [The network rejected an action](action-rejected.md).

## Editing and deleting

The menu of your comment is the **“Actions”** button with three dots:

- **“Edit”** — correct the text and click **“Save”**. An edited comment has a pencil next to its date: **“Edited”**. The network allows a comment to be edited up to 4 times and only within 30 days.
- **“Delete”** — confirm: **“Delete comment?”** The text is replaced with **“Comment was deleted”**.

The author of a post can delete other people’s comments under it — that is how they moderate their discussion.

> [!NOTE]
> A deletion is also a blockchain record: it asks apps not to show the comment. The text itself stays in the blockchain’s history — see [Privacy: what others can see](privacy.md).

## Rating a comment

Under each comment there are 👍 and 👎 with the number of ratings. Click to rate. You rate a comment once and cannot change it, and you cannot rate your own comment. Sometimes the app asks before a dislike: **“Dislike?”**

- A like lifts the comment in the **“Most interesting first”** order and adds a little to the author’s [reputation](reputation.md) if it came from an experienced member.
- A dislike does not affect reputation, but lowers the comment in the **“Most interesting first”** order.

A new account can rate 100 comments a day, a full one 300 — see [Account status and limits](limits.md).

## Comment order

Above an expanded discussion there is **“Sort”**:

- **“Most interesting first”** — comments with likes and replies, from authors with a high reputation and from the post’s author come first, and your own comments are lifted too. If someone wrote many comments under the post, each of them weighs less;
- **“Newest first”**;
- **“Oldest first”**.

The order every discussion opens in is chosen in the [settings](settings.md) — **“Comments order”**. The **“Refresh”** button next to the order reloads the discussion.

## Hidden comments

Some comments are collapsed, with a note instead of the text:

- **“Hidden: user is blocked”** — you have [blocked](blocking.md) the author;
- **“Hidden due to low author reputation”** — the author’s reputation is negative, and the comment has collected 5 or more dislikes.

**“Show anyway”** opens such a comment.

## When you cannot comment

Instead of the input field the app says why:

- **“Sign in to leave a comment”** — you need to [sign in](sign-in.md);
- **“The post author has restricted you from commenting”** — the author has blocked you;
- **“Daily comment limit reached. Try again later.”** — a new account can leave 50 comments a day, a full one 200;
- **“Your reputation is too low to post.”** — your reputation is below −50.
