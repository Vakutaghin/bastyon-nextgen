# Ratings and discussions

How to respond to other people’s posts: ratings, comments, tips, reposts and reports.

## What you can do with someone else’s post

- **Rate** it from one to five stars — see [Rating posts](ratings.md).
- **Comment** on the post or reply to someone’s comment — see [Comments](comments.md).
- **Thank the author** with PKOIN — see [Tipping the author](tips.md).
- **Repost** it to your profile or send a link — see [Reposting and linking to a post](sharing.md).
- **Report** it if it breaks the network’s rules — see [Reports](reports.md).

A guest without an account sees ratings and comments, but to rate, comment and do the rest you need to [sign in](sign-in.md).

## All of these are blockchain records

A rating, a comment, a repost and a report are [transactions](glossary.md#transaction). The app signs them with your account’s key, and they stay on the blockchain forever. It follows that:

- They are public: anyone can see who rated and commented on what, for example in the [block explorer](explorer.md).
- The network takes a minute or two to confirm them — see [Confirming actions](transactions.md).
- Each kind of action has its own daily limit, lower for a new account than for a full one — see [Account status and limits](limits.md).
- A rating cannot be taken back, and a deleted comment stays in the blockchain’s history.

A tip is a PKOIN transfer, and it is public too.
