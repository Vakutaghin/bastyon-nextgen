# Earnings

How people earn PKOIN in Bastyon: tips from readers, network rewards — and where to see them.

## The “Earnings” tab

The wallet’s **“Earnings”** tab shows **“Account earnings”**: how many coins have arrived at your main wallet from others over all time. Three numbers:

- **“From blockchain lottery”** — network rewards from the block lottery, see below;
- **“Received with comments”** — coins people attached to comments under your posts. That could be done in the previous Bastyon app; this app cannot send them that way;
- **“Received as transfers and tips”** — ordinary transfers from other people, including [tips](tips.md).

Your own transfers and the change from your own actions are not counted.

## Tips

The main way to earn as an author is tips from readers. They send them with the gift button on your posts and the **“Tip”** item in the menu of your comments. The coins arrive at your main wallet at once, with no middleman, and you get a **“Tip received”** notification — see [Tipping the author](tips.md).

## The block lottery

With every new block the network issues new coins, and most of the block reward goes to the node that produced it. The lottery distributes the rest.

Nowadays the lottery rewards moderators: if they voted in a jury in the previous block, 10% of the block reward is split between them equally. Who can become a moderator is described in [Moderation and rules](moderation.md). Each such reward comes with a **“Lottery reward”** notification.

<details>
<summary>How the lottery used to work</summary>

Until September 2025 the lottery paid authors: in every block winners were chosen among posts that got 4 and 5 stars from experienced members, and the more such ratings, the higher the chance. Even earlier, authors of liked comments and those who invited authors to the network were rewarded too. Everything you won back then is included in **“From blockchain lottery”**.

The lottery rules are written in the node code [pocketnet.core](https://github.com/pocketnetteam/pocketnet.core) and change only through an update of the network rules.

</details>

## A node

Nodes get the most: they produce blocks and take the main part of the reward. That requires your own node with staked coins — a separate program for a computer or a server; it is not part of this app. What a node is — see [Network and nodes](network.md).
