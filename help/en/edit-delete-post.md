# Editing or deleting a post

How to fix a published post or delete it — and what stays on the blockchain.

## Editing

1. Under your post, click **“Edit”**.
2. **“Edit post”** opens: correct the text, images, tags, visibility or language.
3. Click **“Save”**.

You cannot change the kind of publication (a post cannot become an article or the other way round), the attached video or the poll — they stay as they were.

Network restrictions:

- a post can be edited up to 5 times, an article up to 10, and only within 30 days of publishing;
- no more than once a minute: otherwise the network replies **“You can edit only once per blockchain block. Wait a minute and try again.”**

Readers see the new version once the network confirms the edit. Earlier versions stay on the blockchain and can be found in the [block explorer](explorer.md).

## Deleting

1. Under your post, click **“Delete”**.
2. Confirm: **“Delete this post?”**

The post disappears from feeds, and in its place you immediately see **“Post deleted”**. Deletion cannot be undone.

> [!NOTE]
> A deletion is also a blockchain record: it tells apps to stop showing the post. The post itself and its comments stay in the blockchain's history, like everything that got there — see [Privacy: what others can see](privacy.md).
