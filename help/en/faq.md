# Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions people ask most often.

## What is PKOIN?

[PKOIN](glossary.md#pkoin) is the network's own coin. It pays for actions on the blockchain — boosting posts and tipping authors — and a small balance covers transaction fees. More in [How to get PKOIN](how-to-buy-pkoin.md).

## How do I sign in?

Your account is a 12-word [recovery phrase](glossary.md#recovery-phrase). There is no email, no password and no central server: the phrase is the account. Whoever knows the phrase controls the account, so keep it offline and never share it.

## What if I lose my recovery phrase?

> [!CAUTION]
> It cannot be recovered. Only you hold the keys, and there is no service that could give you access back.

Make a secure backup before you start posting.

## Is it free?

Browsing and posting are free. Some actions spend tiny network fees and need a minimal balance — this protects the network from spam.

## Who moderates content?

Moderation is decentralized. You can block and report accounts, and node operators apply their own rules. Illegal content — above all, child sexual abuse material — is prohibited everywhere on the network.
