# Favorites

How to save a post, a video or a track to come back to it later, and where to find what you saved.

## Saving

- **A post** — the bookmark icon in the top right corner of a post. A filled bookmark means the post is in favorites; click it again to remove it. The bookmark in a post opened in full works the same way.
- **A video or a track** — **“Add to Favorites”** in the three-dot menu, and **“Remove from Favorites”** takes it out. It is the same Favorites as for posts: the bookmark on a post with a video and the menu item mark the same thing.
- The **“Add to…”** window — the **“Favorites”** row at the top.

Favorites are kept in your account data, so you need to be signed in to save.

## Where to find it

- The **“Favorites”** section in the left panel — everything you saved as a feed, the most recent first.
- The **“Favorites”** tab of the [Video section](video-section.md) — videos only, of the [Audio section](audio-section.md) — tracks only, as a grid or a list with **“Play all”**.
- The **“Favorites”** tile at the top of the [Collections and playlists](collections.md) page.

If the author deleted a post, it no longer shows in your favorites.

## Where it is stored

Favorites are a [private list](media-library.md): they are kept in your account data on the chat server and are the same on all your devices. They do not go to the blockchain, so nobody sees what you saved.

There used to be three separate lists: post bookmarks — on this device only, “Saved” videos and “My music”. Now they are one Favorites. Bookmarks that lived on the device move into it by themselves the first time you sign in to the new version, and saved videos and music are already there.

- The **“Clear saved data”** button in the settings does not touch favorites.
- Signing out of an account erases only the copy on the device: sign in again and favorites come back from the server.
