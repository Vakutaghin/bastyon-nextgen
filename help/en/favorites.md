# Favorites

How to save a post to come back to it later, and where to find what you saved.

## Saving a post

Click the bookmark icon in the top right corner of a post. A filled bookmark means the post is in favorites; click it again to remove it. The bookmark in a post opened in full works the same way.

## Where to find it

The **“Favorites”** section in the left panel shows saved posts, the most recently saved first. If the author deleted a post, it is no longer in your favorites.

## Where it is stored

Favorites are stored only on this device and separately for each account. They do not go to the blockchain, so nobody sees what you saved — but they do not move to another device either.

- The **“Clear cache”** button in the settings does not touch favorites.
- Signing out of an account removes its favorites from the device — see [Several accounts and signing out](accounts.md).
