# Categories, tags and sorting

How to tune the feed to your taste: pick categories, add your own tags and show the best of the week.

The filters are in the left panel under the feed sections. They work in the **“Feed”**, **“Subscriptions”**, **“Video”**, **“Audio”**, **“Articles”** and **“Discussed”** sections.

## Categories

A category is a set of tags on one topic: "Cars/Racing", "Science/Technology", "Travel/Architecture" and others, 20 ready-made ones in total — the same as in the old Bastyon. Their tags follow the feed language: in English "Sport" picks posts tagged `sports`, in Russian — posts tagged `спорт`. Click a category to keep only posts with its tags in the feed; click it again to deselect it. You can pick several categories — the feed shows posts from any of them. **“Reset filter”** deselects them all.

**Your own category.** The **“Add category”** button (the plus in the heading) asks for a tag: letters, digits and `_`, without the hash sign; spaces turn into `_` by themselves. A category with this tag appears in the list with a ⭐ icon, and you can remove it with the cross.

## Tags

- **“Trending tags”** — the most popular tags in the feed language. Click a tag to filter the feed by it. **“Reset tags”** deselects them.
- **A tag in a post** also filters the feed: it appears at the top of the category list with a ⚡ icon and disappears after the app restarts.

Selected categories and tags add up: the feed shows posts that have at least one of the selected tags.

## Best first

The **“Best first”** switch above the categories changes the feed order: instead of fresh posts it shows the best of the last week — those with the highest ratings. Categories and tags keep working.

## Feed language

The **“Feed language”** field under the **“Best first”** switch chooses the language of the posts in the feed, the tags and the recommendations. The interface language stays as it is. Changing the interface language, in the header or in the settings, switches the feed to the same language.

**Several languages.** You can tick several languages in the list — the feed shows posts in all of them. Clicking a language ticks or unticks it; the field then shows codes, for example “RU, EN, DE”. Untick them all and the feed is in the interface language again. The node picks posts by one language only, so the app asks it about each language separately and merges the answers: by time in the regular feed, by ratings in **“Best first”**, by the number of comments in **“Discussed”**. With several languages, tags, categories, recommendations and promoted posts are in the interface language if it is among the chosen ones, otherwise in the first of them.

The **“Subscriptions”** section, a person's page and their videos under a video are in all languages: you see the posts of the people you follow whatever language they write in.

In languages with few posts (Korean, Italian, Serbian) the node cannot gather a whole feed page in time. Then the app looks for posts one at a time and says above the feed that it grows slowly. If the node cannot find the next post either, the feed says so. Among several languages such a language does not stop the others: its posts just stop coming.

## Author reputation

The **“Author reputation”** field under **“Feed language”** hides posts by authors whose reputation is below the chosen one: “From 0” removes those with a negative reputation, “From 10” newcomers too, then “From 100”, “From 1,000”, “From 10,000”. **“Custom…”** opens a field for any whole number. **“Any”** shows everyone. An author's reputation is shown in their profile; people who post regularly usually have thousands.

The threshold works in **“Feed”**, **“Video”**, **“Audio”**, **“Articles”** and **“Discussed”** and on promoted posts. **“Subscriptions”** and **“Favorites”** show everyone: you chose those people and posts yourself. Your own posts are never hidden.

The node does not know such a threshold (the network has its own, the same for everyone), so the app filters the posts. When almost a whole feed page is hidden, it fetches the next pages by itself; when everything is hidden for a few hundred posts in a row, the feed stops and offers **“Keep looking”**.

## Where it is remembered

The selected categories, your own categories, the **“Best first”** switch, the **“Feed language”** and the **“Author reputation”** are saved on this device and shared by all accounts on it. On another device you need to pick the filters again.
