# Categories, tags and sorting

How to tune the feed to your taste: pick categories, add your own tags and show the best of the week.

The filters are in the left panel under the feed sections. They work in the **“Feed”**, **“Subscriptions”**, **“Video”**, **“Audio”**, **“Articles”** and **“Discussed”** sections.

## Categories

A category is a set of tags on one topic: "Cars/Racing", "Science/Technology", "Travel/Architecture" and others, 18 ready-made ones in total. Click a category to keep only posts with its tags in the feed; click it again to deselect it. You can pick several categories — the feed shows posts from any of them. **“Reset filter”** deselects them all.

**Your own category.** The **“Add category”** button (the plus in the heading) asks for a tag: letters, digits and `_`, without the hash sign; spaces turn into `_` by themselves. A category with this tag appears in the list with a ⭐ icon, and you can remove it with the cross.

## Tags

- **“Trending tags”** — the most popular tags in your interface language. Click a tag to filter the feed by it. **“Reset tags”** deselects them.
- **A tag in a post** also filters the feed: it appears at the top of the category list with a ⚡ icon and disappears after the app restarts.

Selected categories and tags add up: the feed shows posts that have at least one of the selected tags.

## Best first

The **“Best first”** switch above the categories changes the feed order: instead of fresh posts it shows the best of the last week — those with the highest ratings. Categories and tags keep working.

## Where it is remembered

The selected categories, your own categories and the **“Best first”** switch are saved on this device and shared by all accounts on it. On another device you need to pick the filters again.
