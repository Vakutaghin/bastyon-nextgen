# Getting started

Six steps from installing the app to your first post.

## 1. Install the app

Download Bastyon NextGen for your computer or phone from the releases page — which file to pick and how to install it is covered in [Installing the app](install.md).

## 2. Create an account or sign in

- A new account — the **“Sign up”** button in the header, or in the menu on a phone: pick a nickname, no email or phone number needed. Within a few minutes the network confirms the registration — see [Signing up](create-account.md).
- An account from the previous Bastyon app — the **“Sign in”** button and your 12-word phrase or private key — see [Signing in](sign-in.md).

## 3. Save your recovery phrase

Once the registration is confirmed, the app shows your 12-word [recovery phrase](glossary.md#recovery-phrase). Write it down on paper or save it in a password manager: it is the only way to get your account back if the device is lost. How to keep the phrase and check that your copy is correct is covered in [Recovery phrase and private key](recovery-phrase.md).

## 4. Set up your profile

Add an avatar, a name and a few words about yourself so people recognize you — see [Your profile](edit-profile.md).

## 5. Find interesting people

Browse the [feed](feed.md), pick categories and tags, follow authors — see [Subscriptions](subscriptions.md). Where everything is in the app is explained in [Finding your way around](interface.md).

## 6. Make your first post

Click **“Create post”** above the feed, write some text, add images or a video and publish — see [Publishing](publishing.md). Others will see the post in about a minute, once the network confirms it.

> [!TIP]
> The coins the network sends when you sign up are enough for your first actions. How to top up your balance and raise your limits is covered in [Wallet and PKOIN](wallet.md) and [Account status and limits](limits.md).
