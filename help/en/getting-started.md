# Getting started

Three steps to find your way around Bastyon.

## 1. Create your account

Create a new account and write down your 12-word [recovery phrase](glossary.md#recovery-phrase). Keep it offline — on paper or in a password manager: it is the only way to get your access back.

## 2. Make your first post

Open the post editor to share text, images, video links and tags. You can also comment on other people's posts and boost them.

## 3. Use your wallet

The **“Wallet”** section has your address and QR code for receiving [PKOIN](glossary.md#pkoin), transfers and tips for authors. Where to get coins is explained in [How to get PKOIN](how-to-buy-pkoin.md).
