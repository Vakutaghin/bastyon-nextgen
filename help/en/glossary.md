# Glossary

Terms used in the help and in the app.

## Blockchain

A record of every action on the network — transfers, posts, ratings, subscriptions — shared by the whole network. The network's nodes keep it, and nothing already written can be changed after the fact.

## Recovery phrase

The 12 words your account's keys are derived from. Whoever knows the phrase owns the account. A lost phrase cannot be recovered.

## PKOIN

Bastyon's own coin. It pays fees and tips for authors, and it is spent on boosting posts.
