# How to get PKOIN

PKOIN is the coin that powers actions on the network.

## Why you need it

A small [PKOIN](glossary.md#pkoin) balance covers network fees and unlocks actions like boosting posts and tipping authors. You don't need to buy anything to browse and publish ordinary posts.

## Earn it for free

You can get PKOIN without buying it: publish good posts and collect ratings, or ask someone to send you a tip. Share your address (**“Wallet”** → **“Receive”**) so others can send you coins.

## Buy on an exchange

PKOIN is listed on several cryptocurrency exchanges. Create an account on an exchange that lists PKOIN, buy the coins there and withdraw them to your wallet address.

> [!WARNING]
> Double-check the address before sending: blockchain transfers are irreversible.

## Receiving into your wallet

Open the [wallet](bastyon://wallets), copy your address or show the QR code, and use it as the withdrawal address. The coins appear once the network confirms the transaction.
