# How to get PKOIN

PKOIN is the coin that actions in the network run on. Where to get it, how to receive it at your address and how to sell it.

## Do you need to buy it

Reading, posting, commenting and rating do not require buying anything: the fees are tiny, and the network gives you your first coins at registration. Coins come in handy for sending [tips](tips.md), paying in [mini apps](mini-app-payments.md), or making your account full without reputation — that takes a balance of 50 PKOIN, see [Account status and limits](limits.md).

## Getting it without buying

- **Tips.** Readers can thank you for posts and comments — see [Tipping the author](tips.md).
- **A transfer from someone you know.** Give your address to a person who has PKOIN — how to find it is described below.
- **Moderator work and a node.** Moderators get part of the block reward for their jury votes, and node owners for producing blocks, see [Earnings](earnings.md).

## The “Buy/Sell” tab

Everything for buying and selling is in the [wallet](bastyon://wallets?tab=buy), on the **“Buy/Sell”** tab. The link under the balances leads there too.

- **Rate and calculator.** A chart of the PKOIN price in dollars and PKOIN ⇄ USD conversion at the exchange rate from CoinGecko. Sellers set their own rates.
- **Your receiving address** — right away, with a QR code and a **“Copy”** button.
- **Exchanges** — links to PKOIN/USDT trading.
- **From sellers** — PKOIN Exchange and `#pkoin_commerce` offers.
- **Useful links** — sending, transfer history, your address in the blockchain explorer, earnings, account status and this article.

## On an exchange

According to CoinGecko as of November 2026, PKOIN trades against USDT on the **Tapbit** and **DigiFinex** exchanges. The list of exchanges changes — see the current one on the Pocketcoin page at [coingecko.com](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/pocketcoin).

- **To buy.** Open an account on the exchange, buy PKOIN for USDT and withdraw it to your Bastyon wallet address.
- **To sell.** Send PKOIN to the exchange’s deposit address — on the wallet’s **“Transfers”** tab — and sell it there.

Trading volume on the exchanges is small: there are few orders in the order book, and a large purchase or sale will move the price noticeably. A large amount is better bought in parts or from sellers.

## From sellers

- **PKOIN Exchange** is a built-in [mini app](mini-apps.md) for exchange. Sellers state their price, currencies, payment methods, limits and payment time. You create an order, send the money to the seller’s payment details, and the seller confirms the payment and sends the PKOIN. The mini app has access to your account, signature and chat from the start: the seller receives the order as a chat message.
- **`#pkoin_commerce` offers** are posts offering to buy and sell PKOIN. You can find them by searching `#pkoin_commerce` or in the “PKOIN/Peer-to-peer” category in the sidebar. Your own offer is a post with this tag; other tags, a link or a video cannot be added to such a post.

> [!CAUTION]
> The network does not guarantee person-to-person deals: neither PKOIN Exchange nor the offers have a deposit or an arbiter. Look at the seller’s reputation and account age, and do not pay strangers in advance. When selling, check that the money has arrived at your bank, not a picture of a receipt: a receipt is easy to fake. PKOIN transfers cannot be reversed.

Services from the previous Bastyon app — pkoin.net (exchange for other cryptocurrencies), Swipelux (by card) and WPKOIN — are not shown in the new app yet: in November 2026 exchange on pkoin.net did not work, and we are still checking Swipelux and WPKOIN.

## Receiving into your wallet

1. Open the [wallet](bastyon://wallets?tab=buy) and the **“Buy/Sell”** tab — the address and QR code are right there. The address is also on the **“Transfers”** tab: **“Receive”** → **“Show receive address”**.
2. Copy the address with **“Copy”** or show the QR code — and enter it as the withdrawal address on the exchange or send it to the seller.

> [!WARNING]
> Double-check the address before withdrawing: blockchain transfers are irreversible. A Bastyon address starts with a Latin P — coins sent to an address of another network will not arrive.

The coins appear on your balance once the network confirms the transfer, usually within a minute or two. An exchange may hold a withdrawal longer on its side.
