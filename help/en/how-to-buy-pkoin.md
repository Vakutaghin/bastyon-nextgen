# How to get PKOIN

PKOIN is the coin that actions in the network run on. Where to get it and how to receive it at your address.

## Do you need to buy it

Reading, posting, commenting and rating do not require buying anything: the fees are tiny, and the network gives you your first coins at registration. Coins come in handy for sending [tips](tips.md), paying in [mini apps](mini-app-payments.md), or making your account full without reputation — that takes a balance of 50 PKOIN, see [Account status and limits](limits.md).

## Getting it without buying

- **Tips.** Readers can thank you for posts and comments — see [Tipping the author](tips.md).
- **A transfer from someone you know.** Give your address to a person who has PKOIN — how to find it is described below.
- **Moderator work and a node.** Moderators get part of the block reward for their jury votes, and node owners for producing blocks, see [Earnings](earnings.md).

## Buying

According to CoinGecko as of September 2026, PKOIN trades against USDT on the **Tapbit** and **DigiFinex** exchanges. Open an account on the exchange, buy the coins and withdraw them to your Bastyon wallet address. The list of exchanges changes — see the current one on the Pocketcoin page at [coingecko.com](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/pocketcoin).

Two more ways mentioned in the previous Bastyon app’s help:

- the [pkoin.net](https://pkoin.net) service — buying PKOIN for other cryptocurrencies;
- person-to-person exchange: offers to buy and sell PKOIN are published in Bastyon with the `pkoin_commerce` tag, and you can find them by searching `#pkoin_commerce`. Such deals are at your own risk: the network does not guarantee them.

> [!WARNING]
> Double-check the address before withdrawing: blockchain transfers are irreversible. A Bastyon address starts with a Latin P — coins sent to an address of another network will not arrive.

## Receiving into your wallet

1. Open the [wallet](bastyon://wallets) and the **“Transfers”** tab.
2. Choose **“Receive”** and click **“Show receive address”**.
3. Copy the address with **“Copy”** or show the QR code — and enter it as the withdrawal address on the exchange or service.

The coins appear on your balance once the network confirms the transfer, usually within a minute or two. An exchange may hold a withdrawal longer on its side.
