# Finding your way around

The header, the side panels and the menus: where everything is on a computer and on a phone.

## The header

From left to right:

- **The logo** takes you to the home page; the interface language is chosen next to it.
- **Search** — people, posts and tags, see [Search](search.md).
- **Tor** and **IPFS** — status icons of [Tor](tor.md) and [the IPFS module](ipfs-module.md). They work in the desktop app; in other versions the icon only tells you so.
- **The hourglass** appears when you have actions the network has not confirmed yet — see [Confirming actions](transactions.md).
- **The bell** — **“Notifications”**, see [Notifications](notifications.md).
- **The bug** — **“Report a bug”**, see [Reporting a bug](report-bug.md).
- **The speech bubble** opens the messenger; the number on it is unread messages.
- **The sun or moon** switches between the light and dark theme.
- **Your avatar** opens the account menu. Until you sign in, the **“Sign in”** and **“Sign up”** buttons are there instead.

## Above the feed

- **“Refresh feed”** — load the latest posts.
- **“Create post”** — open the [post editor](post-composer.md).
- The buttons at the edges collapse the left panel (**“Collapse menu”**) and hide the right one (**“Hide sidebar”**). The app remembers how you left them.

## The left panel

Feed and app sections:

- **“Feed”**, **“Subscriptions”** (once you are signed in), **“Video”**, **“Audio”**, **“Articles”**, **“Favorites”**, **“Discussed”** — see [Feed](feed.md);
- **“Explorer”** — the [block explorer](explorer.md);
- **“Help”** — this help;
- **“Mini-apps”** — see [Mini apps](mini-apps.md); pinned mini apps are listed below.

Under the sections are the **“Best first”** switch, **“Categories”** and **“Trending tags”**, which select the posts in the feed — see [Categories, tags and sorting](feed-filters.md).

## The right panel

- **“Who to follow”** — authors worth following.
- **“Latest comments”** — fresh comments from the whole network, refreshed once a minute. Click a comment to open its post.

## The account menu

Click your avatar in the header:

- **“Profile”** — your page, see [Your profile](edit-profile.md);
- **“Wallets”** — see [Wallet and PKOIN](wallet.md);
- **“Limits”** — see [Account status and limits](limits.md);
- **“My videos”** and **“My files”** — the latter only in the desktop app, see [My files](my-files.md);
- **“Help”** and **“Settings”**;
- **“Switch account”** and **“Sign out”** — see [Several accounts and signing out](accounts.md).

If the registration was interrupted, the menu gets a **“Finish registration”** item.

## The messenger

The speech bubble in the header or the round button in the bottom right corner opens the chat window over the feed. The feed stays in place, so you can read and chat at the same time. The arrow in the window's title goes back to the chat list, the cross closes the window. On a narrow screen chats open full screen. More in [Messenger](messenger.md).

## On a phone and in a narrow window

When the screen is narrow — on a phone or in a shrunk window on a computer — there are no side panels, and a bar appears at the bottom: **“Home”**, **“Search”**, **“Apps”**, **“Chats”** and **“Wallet”**. The other sections are in the menu behind the three-line button on the left of the header: **“Feed”**, **“Video”**, **“Wallet”**, **“Settings”** and **“Help”**. More in [The phone app](mobile-app.md).
