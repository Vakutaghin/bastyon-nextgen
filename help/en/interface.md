# Finding your way around

The header, the side panels and the menus: where everything is on a computer and on a phone.

## The header

From left to right:

- **The logo** takes you to the home page; the interface language is chosen next to it, and the feed switches to it too.
- The **“Back”** and **“Forward”** arrows step through the pages you opened, like browser buttons. While there is nowhere to go, the arrow is pale. From the keyboard — Alt+← and Alt+→, on a Mac — Cmd+[ and Cmd+], see [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md).
- **Search** — people, posts and tags, see [Search](search.md).

To the right of the search are the icons. This is how they stand until you rearrange them yourself:

- **“Services”** — mini apps inside Bastyon: music, video, games, PKOIN exchange, see [Services](mini-apps.md). In a narrow window only the icon is left of the button.
- **The hourglass** appears when you have actions the network has not confirmed yet — see [Confirming actions](transactions.md). It always sits next to the bell.
- **The bell** — **“Notifications”**, see [Notifications](notifications.md).
- **The speech bubble** opens the messenger; the number on it is unread messages.
- **Tor** and **IPFS** — status icons of [Tor](tor.md) and [the IPFS module](ipfs-module.md). Only in the desktop app.
- **The sun or moon** switches between the light and dark theme.
- **Your avatar** opens the account menu. Until you sign in, the **“Sign in”** and **“Sign up”** buttons are there instead; on a phone only **“Sign in”** — signing up is in the menu there.

The bug icon — **“Report a bug”** — can be added to the header; by default it is an item of the account menu. Which icons to show and in what order is chosen in the settings, see [Header and panels](settings.md#header-and-panels).

## Above the feed

- **“Refresh feed”** — load the latest posts.
- The **“Services”** shelf — popular mini apps in one row, see [Services](mini-apps.md).
- **“Create post”** — open the [post editor](post-composer.md).
- The buttons at the edges collapse the left panel (**“Collapse menu”**) and hide the right one (**“Hide sidebar”**). The app remembers how you left them.

## The left panel

Feed and app sections:

- **“Feed”**, **“Subscriptions”** (once you are signed in), **“Video”**, **“Audio”**, **“Articles”**, **“Favorites”**, **“Discussed”** — see [Feed](feed.md);
- **“Services”** — see [Services](mini-apps.md); pinned mini apps are listed below, under the line;
- **“Top accounts”** — the accounts with the highest reputation in the network, see [Top accounts](top-accounts.md);
- **“Blockchain explorer”** — [blocks, transactions, addresses and the node network map](explorer.md);
- **“Help”** — this help.

Under the sections are the **“Best first”** switch, the **“Feed language”**, **“Categories”** and **“Trending tags”**, which select the posts in the feed — see [Categories, tags and sorting](feed-filters.md).

The sections and the blocks below them can be hidden and reordered — see [Header and panels](settings.md#header-and-panels).

## The right panel

- **“Who to follow”** — authors worth following.
- **“Latest comments”** — fresh comments from the whole network, refreshed once a minute. Click a comment to open its post.

## The account menu

Click your avatar in the header. By default the menu has:

- **“Profile”** — your page, see [Your profile](edit-profile.md);
- **“Wallets”** — see [Wallet and PKOIN](wallet.md);
- **“Paid subscriptions”** — see [Paid subscriptions](paid-subscriptions.md);
- **“Media studio”** — uploading video and audio, your clips, their covers and statistics, see [Media studio](media-studio.md);
- **“My files”** — only in the desktop app, see [My files](my-files.md);
- **“Limits”** — see [Account status and limits](limits.md);
- **“Mesh networks”** — in the desktop app and on Android, see [Mesh networks](mesh.md);
- **“Help”**, **“Report a bug”** — see [Reporting a bug](report-bug.md) — and **“Settings”**;
- **“Switch account”** and **“Sign out”** — see [Several accounts and signing out](accounts.md).

Items can be hidden and reordered; only **“Settings”** cannot be hidden — see [Header and panels](settings.md#header-and-panels). **“Switch account”** and **“Sign out”** are always at the bottom. If the registration was interrupted, the menu gets a **“Finish registration”** item.

## The messenger

The speech bubble in the header or the round button in the bottom right corner opens the chat window over the feed. The feed stays in place, so you can read and chat at the same time. The arrow in the window's title goes back to the chat list, the cross closes the window. On a narrow screen there is no speech bubble in the header: **“Chats”** in the bottom bar opens chats full screen. More in [Messenger](messenger.md).

## On a phone and in a narrow window

When the screen is narrow — on a phone or in a shrunk window on a computer — there are no side panels, and a bar appears at the bottom: **“Home”**, **“Search”**, **“Chats”**, **“Wallet”** and **“Services”**. Tapping the section you are in takes the page back to the top.

The header slides away as you scroll down and comes back as soon as you scroll up a little. While the on-screen keyboard is open, the bottom bar is hidden to leave room for the text.

The menu behind the three-line button in the header, right of the logo, is the same left panel as on a computer: the feed sections, **“Services”**, **“Blockchain explorer”**, **“Help”**, pinned apps, and the category and tag filters. When you are signed in, **“Account”** follows: the same items as in the account menu on a computer, in the same order, except **“Help”**, which is already above. Otherwise **“Sign in”** and **“Sign up”** are at the top of the menu.

Of the icons, only the bell and the theme switch fit in the phone header; if you hide them in the settings, they are gone here too.

To the right of the menu button, the **“Back”** arrow appears when there is somewhere to go back to; a narrow screen has no **“Forward”** arrow. On Android, the system Back button or gesture also goes back.

On a phone posts take the full width of the screen, without frames: pictures and videos run edge to edge, with a thin line between posts. A post opened in full takes the whole screen.

A phone turned sideways works the same way, except that the section bar becomes a column at the left edge and the header gets lower: there is little height. Pictures and videos in the feed are no taller than the screen, windows fit on the screen, and chats show the list and the conversation side by side when the screen is wide enough. A tablet turned sideways gets the computer layout: side panels and the chat window in the corner.

Without an account, **“Chats”**, **“Wallet”** and **“Subscriptions”** open the sign-in window, and after you sign in, the section itself. More in [The phone app](mobile-app.md).
