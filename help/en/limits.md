# Account status and limits

How many posts, ratings and comments you can make per day, how a trial account differs from a full one, and how to raise your limits.

Signing up to Bastyon needs neither an email nor a phone number, so the network protects itself from bots in another way: new accounts can do less, and their options grow with reputation or balance. Every node checks the limits, so they cannot be bypassed in the app.

## The Limits page

Open the account menu (your avatar in the header) → **“Limits”**. The **“My limits”** page shows:

- **“Reputation”** — see [Reputation](reputation.md);
- **“Status”** — **“Trial”** or **“Top”** (a full account);
- how many actions are left today and how many are allowed in total: posts, video and audio posts, articles, stars, comments, comment ratings and reports.

## Trial and full accounts

A new account is a [trial](glossary.md#trial-account) one. It becomes full when one of the conditions is met:

- a reputation of 100 or more;
- 50 PKOIN or more on the account's address.

The condition is checked on every action: if the balance drops below 50 PKOIN and the reputation is under 100, the account becomes a trial one again.

| In the last 24 hours | Trial | Full account |
|---|---|---|
| Posts | 5 | 30 |
| Video posts | 5 | 30 |
| Audio posts | 5 | 30 |
| Articles | 1 | 3 |
| Post ratings | 15 | 200 |
| Comments | 50 | 200 |
| Comment ratings | 100 | 300 |
| Reports | 30 | 30 |

With a balance of 250 PKOIN or more you can publish up to 100 video posts a day.

What else sets a trial account apart:

- It can only publish for everyone: a trial account gets a single visibility option in the post settings — **“Trial accounts can only post for everyone”**.
- It cannot give 1–3 stars, only 4 and 5.
- Posts can be rated no earlier than 24 hours after signing up.

> [!NOTE]
> Only experienced accounts can send reports, whatever their status — see [Reports](reports.md).

## When a limit comes back

A day is not a calendar day but a sliding window: each action stops counting 24 hours after it landed on the blockchain. If you wrote 5 posts in the morning, by the same time tomorrow the limit is fully back.

There are other restrictions too:

- you can change your profile up to 10 times a day and no more than once a minute;
- a post can be edited up to 5 times, an article up to 10 times, and only within 30 days of publishing;
- a comment can be edited up to 4 times within 30 days.

## Limit reached

When the network rejects an action because of a limit, the app says so, for example: "You have reached your post limit for the last 24 hours". If some limit is used up and the account lacks the balance or reputation to become full, the **“Limits”** page shows a **“Limit reached”** block with the **“Increase limits”** button. It opens the wallet: topping up the balance is the quickest way, and where to get PKOIN is covered in [How to get PKOIN](how-to-buy-pkoin.md). The other way is reputation: it grows with good ratings of your posts and comments.
