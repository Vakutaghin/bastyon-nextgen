# If you lost access

What to do if you lost your phone, forgot your passphrase or cannot find your 12 words.

## You have the phrase but not the device

Install the app on a new device and sign in with your recovery phrase — see [Signing in](sign-in.md). Everything comes back: the profile, posts, subscriptions, coins and conversations, because they are stored on the network, not on the device. Only drafts and favorites are gone — they lived on the old device.

If the device may have been stolen, think about whether someone could use it:

- **A [passphrase](device-protection.md) was set** — without it the keys on the device cannot be decrypted, so there is nothing to worry about.
- **There was no passphrase** — whoever has the device can open the app as you.

An account's keys cannot be changed: the address is the key. So if there is a risk that someone has your key, create a new account and move all your coins to its address. You can tell your followers about the move in a post from the old account.

## You forgot your passphrase

In the **“Unlock your wallet”** window click **“Forgot your passphrase?”** → **“Restore with your 12-word recovery phrase”**. The accounts saved on this device are removed, and you need to sign in again with the phrase. Coins and posts are not affected — they are on the blockchain. More in [Protection on this device](device-protection.md).

## You do not have the phrase

- **The app is still open on some device.** Right away open **“Settings”** → **“Private key”** → **“Reveal private key”**, write the phrase down and check your copy — see [Recovery phrase and private key](recovery-phrase.md).
- **The phrase is nowhere.** It cannot be recovered: nobody has a copy, including the developers. All that is left is to create a new account.

## "Local wallet key is lost"

This window appears when the app cannot decrypt the saved accounts: the browser cleared its storage or the data is damaged. There are two options:

- **“Restore with your 12-word recovery phrase”** → **“Clear and restore”** — the data on this device is cleared, and you sign in again with the phrase.
- **“Later”** — everything stays as it is, and the app works without signing in. Useful if the phrase is not at hand right now.
