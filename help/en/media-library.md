# Private lists

Saved, Watch later, My music and private playlists — only you see them, and they are the same on all your devices.

## What there is

- **“Saved”** and **“Watch later”** — videos, tabs of the [Video section](video-section.md). Newest on top.
- **“My music”** — tracks, a tab of the [Audio section](audio-section.md). Newest on top.
- Private playlists — video or audio, on the section's **“Playlists”** tab. You set the order; new entries go to the end.

Entries are added from a video's or track's menu and from the **“Add to…”** window — see [Collections and playlists](collections.md). **“New private playlist”** creates one — on the Playlists tab or right in that window. A private playlist's page has **“Rename”** and **“Delete”**; deleting removes only the playlist, the entries stay.

Any list plays in a row: **“Play all”** or **“Shuffle”**.

## Where they are kept

Not in the blockchain but in your account data on the chat server — next to the list of [messenger channels](channels.md). That means:

- nobody but you sees them. Only the chat server does, as with your list of chats;
- edits are free and unlimited, unlike public playlists;
- the lists are the same on every device signed in to this account. Changes made on two devices at once are not lost: each entry is compared separately.

Until the messenger connects (no network, the server is unreachable), changes are kept on this device and go to the server as soon as there is a connection.

A list holds up to 1,000 entries, and there can be up to 100 private playlists.

[Favorites](favorites.md) are different: they are kept on this device only.
