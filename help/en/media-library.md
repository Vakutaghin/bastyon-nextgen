# Private lists

Favorites, Watch later, private playlists and collections — only you see them, and they are the same on all your devices.

## What there is

- **“Favorites”** — everything you marked with the bookmark on a post or with **“Add to Favorites”** in a video's or track's menu. Newest on top. See [Favorites](favorites.md).
- **“Watch later”** — videos, a tab of the [Video section](video-section.md). Newest on top.
- Private playlists — video or audio, and private collections — any posts. You set the order; new entries go to the end.

A private list is created by the **“Create”** window on the Collections and playlists page or by **“New list…”** in the **“Add to…”** window, with Who sees it set to **“Only me”** — see [Create](collections.md#create). Entries are added from a video's or track's menu and from the Add to… window.

A private list's page has **“Rename”**, **“Make public”** and **“Delete”**; deleting removes only the list, the entries stay. Make public moves the list to the blockchain, where everyone sees it — see [Making it private or public](collections.md#making-it-private-or-public).

A playlist plays in a row: **“Play all”** or **“Shuffle”**. A private collection shows its posts as a feed.

All private lists, together with public ones, are on the **“Collections”** page in the left panel — see [All of yours on one page](collections.md#all-of-yours-on-one-page).

## Where they are kept

Not in the blockchain but in your account data on the chat server — next to the list of [messenger channels](channels.md). That means:

- nobody but you sees them. Only the chat server does, as with your list of chats;
- edits are free and unlimited, unlike public lists;
- the lists are the same on every device signed in to this account. Changes made on two devices at once are not lost: each entry is compared separately.

Until the messenger connects (no network, the server is unreachable), changes are kept on this device and go to the server as soon as there is a connection.

A list holds up to 1,000 entries, and there can be up to 100 private playlists and collections.

Versions of the app before 0.32 don’t know Favorites and private collections. If you use one on another device, update it: when the old version saves its lists, it erases the new ones from the server, and they come back only when the new version opens.
