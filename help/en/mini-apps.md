# Mini apps

Third-party apps inside Bastyon: how to find, open, pin and delete them.

## Where they are

**“Mini-apps”** in the left panel; on a phone, **“Apps”** in the bottom bar. The page has two sections:

- **“Installed”** — apps that come with Bastyon: Barteron, WPKOIN, Swipelux, PKOIN Exchange, Block Explorer and the Bastyon documentation — plus the ones you loaded yourself, see [Your own mini app](mini-apps-dev.md);
- **“Catalog”** — apps developers have published on the Bastyon network.

You can search by name with the **“Search apps…”** field, or pick a category above the list.

## Opening and closing

Click an app, and it opens inside Bastyon, filling the page. Catalog apps need no installing: they open right away.

To close it, use the **“Close”** tab in the top right corner. The app asks again: “Unsaved data in the app may be lost.” — **“Close”** or **“Stay”**. After closing, the feed opens.

If an app takes too long to load, you see “… is not responding — try again later”: the app’s site is down or overloaded.

## Favorites

The star on a card is **“Add to favorites”**: the app appears in the left panel, one click away. Barteron is always there. To remove it, press the star again — **“Remove from favorites”**.

## Deleting

Only an app you loaded yourself can be deleted: **“Delete”** on its card. All permissions granted to it are deleted with it. Apps that come with Bastyon cannot be deleted, and catalog apps are not installed in the first place.

## Who makes them

Mini apps are made by third-party developers and run on their own sites — Bastyon only shows them in a window. An app knows about you only what you allowed it — see [Mini app permissions](mini-app-permissions.md). It cannot pay on your behalf: you confirm every payment yourself, see [Payments in mini apps](mini-app-payments.md).

Like any site, the app’s site sees your IP address. That is why mini apps do not open while [Tor](tor.md) is on: they load directly, bypassing Tor.
