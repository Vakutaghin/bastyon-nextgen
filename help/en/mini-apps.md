# Services

Mini apps inside Bastyon — music and radio, video, games, learning, PKOIN exchange: how to find, open, pin and delete them.

## Where they are

- **“Services”** in the header, next to search, and in the left panel; on a phone, **“Services”** in the bottom bar.
- The **“Services”** shelf above the feed: the most popular apps in one row; **“All services”** leads to the catalog. If you do not need the shelf, press the cross — **“Hide from the feed”**. You can bring it back at the bottom of the Services page: **“Show services in the feed”**. While [Tor](tor.md) is on, there is no shelf.

The **“Services”** page has the **“Popular”** section — apps developers have published on the Bastyon network, the ones users rate higher first.

If you loaded an app of your own, it is in the **“Your apps”** section, see [Your own mini app](mini-apps-dev.md).

You can search by name and description with the **“Search apps…”** field, or pick a section above the list: **“Music & radio”**, **“Video”**, **“Games”**, **“Learning”** and others. Only sections with something in them are shown.

## Opening and closing

Click an app, and it opens inside Bastyon, filling the page. Catalog apps need no installing: they open right away.

To close it, use the button with a cross at the right edge near the top. For the first seconds after opening it shows the **“Close”** label, then only the cross stays and the label appears on hover. The app asks again: “Unsaved data in the app may be lost.” — **“Close”** or **“Stay”**. After closing you return to where you opened the app from — the catalog, the feed or a profile. If the app did not open or was not found, the button closes it at once, without asking.

If an app takes too long to load, you see “… is not responding — try again later”: the app’s site is down or overloaded.

If you change the theme or the language, an open app learns about it and can switch along with Bastyon.

## Favorites

The star on a card is **“Add to favorites”**: the app appears in the left panel, one click away. Barteron is always there. To remove it, press the star again — **“Remove from favorites”**.

## Barteron

In Barteron you can publish, edit and delete offers, save your profile, write reviews and give ratings: all of it goes to the network on your behalf, as in the previous Bastyon. Offers still waiting for the network to confirm them already show in the Barteron profile. Only Barteron can publish this way; other mini apps cannot.

An offer can carry a video. The upload window is the same as in the post editor: picking a file, upload progress, cancel, retry and cover. The video uploads to a Bastyon video server, into your channel.

## Share on Bastyon

The “Share on Bastyon” button in a mini app opens the post editor with the app’s text, images and link, and the app’s name among the tags. You can change all of it before publishing. Your own post draft stays untouched.

## Deleting

Only an app you loaded yourself can be deleted: **“Delete”** on its card. All permissions granted to it are deleted with it. Catalog apps are not installed in the first place.

## Who makes them

Mini apps are made by third-party developers and run on their own sites — Bastyon only shows them in a window. An app knows about you only what you allowed it — see [Mini app permissions](mini-app-permissions.md). It cannot pay on your behalf: you confirm every payment yourself, see [Payments in mini apps](mini-app-payments.md).

Like any site, the app’s site sees your IP address. That is why mini apps do not open while [Tor](tor.md) is on: they load directly, bypassing Tor.

If you come across something in an app that breaks the rules, report it with the app’s own button, see [Reports](reports.md#reporting-in-a-mini-app).
