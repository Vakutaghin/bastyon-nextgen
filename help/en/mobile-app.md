# The phone app

Bastyon NextGen on Android: installing, the bottom bar, the camera, background playback and what the phone does not have.

## Installing and updating

The app for Android 7 and newer is an .apk file on the releases page, see [Installing the app](install.md). It is not on Google Play yet, so the phone asks you to allow installs from this source.

A new version cannot be installed over the old one: the old one has to be removed first, and the account keys go with it. Before updating, make sure your recovery phrase is written down — see [Updates](updates.md).

## Navigation

The bottom of the screen has a bar: **“Home”**, **“Search”**, **“Apps”**, **“Chats”** and **“Wallet”**. The other sections are in the menu behind the three-line button in the header. More in [Finding your way around](interface.md).

## What the phone can do

- **Video and audio in the background.** A video keeps playing when the app is in the background or the screen is off, and you can control it from the notification, see [Watching videos](video-player.md).
- **Camera and microphone** — to [sign in with a QR code](sign-in.md) and record [voice messages](voice-messages.md). The phone asks for permission the first time.
- **“Share”** — a link to a post can be sent to any app through the system menu, see [Reposting and linking to a post](sharing.md).
- **IPFS files** are saved through the system share menu, see [Opening an IPFS link](open-ipfs-links.md).

Notifications about new events arrive while the app is open — see [Notifications](notifications.md).

## What the phone does not have

- [Tor](tor.md);
- sharing files over IPFS — opening IPFS links works;
- [voice input](voice-input.md);
- [compressing a video](video-compression.md) before upload.

All of these are in the [desktop app](desktop-app.md).
