# Notifications

Who rated you, commented or subscribed: where to see notifications and how to choose what to be told about.

## The bell

The bell in the header is there once you are signed in. The number on it is how many new notifications you have not seen yet. Click it to open the **“Notifications”** list: new ones are highlighted until you close the list.

Each notification shows the kind of event, the time, who did it and the details: the stars of a rating, the text of a comment, the beginning of a post, an amount. Click a notification to go:

- to the post — for ratings, comments, replies, reposts, boosts and new posts; the post opens in a window over the page;
- to the person’s profile — for subscriptions;
- to the wallet — for transfers and rewards.

The three-dot button on a notification is **“Hide notification”**, and **“Clear all”** at the top hides the whole list. Hidden notifications do not come back.

## What you are notified about

- ratings of your posts;
- comments on your posts and replies to your comments;
- ratings of your comments — a like or a dislike;
- new subscribers. **“Private subscription”** means someone subscribed to you and turned on the bell, that is, wants notifications about your posts;
- reposts of your posts and boosts — when someone promotes your post for PKOIN;
- new posts by people whose bell you turned on in their profile — see [Subscriptions](subscriptions.md);
- incoming transfers from 0.05 PKOIN — **“PKOIN received”** with the amount. The app does not show smaller transfers, so as not to let spam through;
- rewards from the network lottery — **“Lottery reward”**, see [Earnings](earnings.md).

Unsubscriptions are not shown in the list.

## When they arrive

While the app is open, it checks for new events every 30 seconds. A new notification also pops up for a few seconds with a sound — no more than two at a time.

When the app is closed, notifications do not arrive: it has no push notifications. On the next start it asks for what was missed — up to the 30 latest events of each kind — and shows them in the list.

The list is kept on this device, separately for each account: the latest 300 notifications.

## What to show

**“Settings”** → the **“Notifications”** tab — **“Notification filter”**. A kind of event that is switched off disappears both from the list and from the pop-ups:

- **“Sound”** — a sound for new notifications;
- **“Browser notifications”** — see below;
- **“Coinstake win”** — lottery rewards;
- **“Transaction received”** — incoming transfers;
- **“New rating (likes)”** — ratings of your posts;
- **“New negative rating”** — 1 and 2 stars for a post and dislikes of comments. Off by default;
- **“New comment”** — comments on your posts;
- **“New reply to comment”** — replies to you;
- **“New follower”** — subscriptions to you;
- **“Comment rating”** — ratings of your comments.

## System notifications

If you turn on **“Browser notifications”**, the app asks the system for permission to show notifications. Then, while its window is minimised or covered by another window, the system itself tells you about new events. This does not work everywhere: the built-in browser of some systems does not support such notifications, and then the switch changes nothing.

## The hourglass is not a notification

On a computer an hourglass sometimes appears next to the bell: these are your own actions still waiting for the network’s confirmation — see [Confirming actions](transactions.md).
