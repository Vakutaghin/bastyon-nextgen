# Notifications

Who rated you, commented or subscribed: where to see notifications and how to choose what to be told about.

## The bell

The bell in the header is there once you are signed in. The number on it is how many new notifications you have not seen yet. Click it to open the **“Notifications”** list: new ones are highlighted until you close the list. On a phone and in a narrow window the list opens full screen; the cross at the top right closes it.

Each notification shows the kind of event, the time, who did it and the details: the stars of a rating, the text of a comment, the beginning of a post, an amount. Click a notification to go where it points:

- to the comment — for comments, replies and ratings of your comments. The post opens in a window over the page, scrolls to the comment and highlights it; for a reply in a thread it expands the thread first;
- to the post — for post ratings, reposts, boosts and new posts;
- to the person’s profile — for subscriptions;
- to the wallet — for transfers and rewards.

While the post or comment is loading, a spinner turns where the time was, and the window opens once it arrives. If the comment has been deleted, the app says so.

The avatar and the name in a notification lead to the profile of whoever did it.

The list starts with the last 20 notifications; **“Show more”** at the bottom adds the next ones.

The three-dot button on a notification has **“Open”**, the same as clicking the notification itself, **“On blockchain”**, the event’s transaction in a window over the page (see [Blockchain explorer](explorer.md#on-blockchain-without-leaving-the-feed)), and **“Hide notification”**. **“Clear all”** at the top hides the whole list, and with a section selected — only that section. Hidden notifications do not come back.

## Sections

Above the list are **“All”** and the sections that currently have something:

- **“Comments”** — comments on your posts and replies to you, the things you can reply to;
- **“Ratings”** — ratings of your posts and comments;
- **“Followers”** — who followed or unfollowed you;
- **“PKOIN”** — transfers, donations, paid subscription payments and network rewards;
- **“Posts”** — reposts and boosts of your posts, new posts of authors whose bell you turned on.

The number on a button is how many new ones the section has. Mentions and profile updates are only in **“All”**.

## Comments

A comment or a reply has a **“Reply”** button. The post opens at that comment with the reply field already open, ready for typing. A reply to a reply already starts with the author’s name, for example “@alice,”, so that everyone in the thread sees whom you are answering. More in [Comments](comments.md).

If you have already replied to a comment, **“You replied”** shows under it — whether you replied from this device or another one: the app looks at the comment’s thread on the network. That way you see which comments are still waiting for an answer. A reply sent from another device may take a few minutes to show up here.

## What you are notified about

- ratings of your posts;
- comments on your posts and replies to your comments. If coins are attached to a comment, the notification shows the amount;
- ratings of your comments — a like or a dislike;
- new subscribers. **“Subscription with notifications”** means someone subscribed to you and turned on the bell, that is, wants notifications about your posts. It is a regular free subscription, not a paid one;
- reposts of your posts and boosts — when someone promotes your post for PKOIN;
- new posts by people whose bell you turned on in their profile — see [Subscriptions](subscriptions.md);
- incoming transfers from 0.05 PKOIN — **“PKOIN received”** with the amount and the sender’s message, if they wrote one, tips — **“Tip received”**, and payments for a [paid subscription](paid-subscriptions.md) — **“Subscription paid”**. The app does not show smaller transfers, so as not to let spam through;
- rewards from the network lottery — **“Lottery reward”**: nowadays moderators get them for their jury votes, see [Earnings](earnings.md).

Unsubscriptions are not shown in the list.

## When they arrive

While the app is open, it checks for new events every 30 seconds. A new notification also pops up for a few seconds with a sound — no more than two at a time. Click a pop-up notification to go where it points, as in the list.

The sound depends on the event. When coins arrive — a transfer, a tip, coins with a comment, a subscription payment, a lottery reward — you hear a coin; for everything else, a short chime. The coin also plays when you send PKOIN yourself: a transfer, a tip, a boost, a subscription or mini-app payment. A batch of events makes one sound.

When the app is closed, notifications do not arrive: it has no push notifications. On the next start it asks for what was missed — up to the 30 latest events of each kind — and shows them in the list.

The list is kept on this device, separately for each account: the latest 300 notifications.

## What to show

**“Settings”** → the **“Notifications”** tab — **“Notification filter”**. A kind of event that is switched off disappears both from the list and from the pop-ups:

- **“Sound”** — all the app’s sounds: notifications, new chat messages and the coin when you send PKOIN;
- **“Browser notifications”** — see below;
- **“Coinstake win”** — lottery rewards;
- **“Transaction received”** — incoming transfers;
- **“New rating (likes)”** — ratings of your posts;
- **“New negative rating”** — 1 and 2 stars for a post and dislikes of comments. Off by default;
- **“New comment”** — comments on your posts;
- **“New reply to comment”** — replies to you;
- **“New follower”** — subscriptions to you;
- **“Comment rating”** — ratings of your comments.

## System notifications

If you turn on **“Browser notifications”**, the app asks the system for permission to show notifications. Then, while its window is minimised or covered by another window, the system itself tells you about new events. Clicking such a notification brings up the app window, and if it is about a single event, also takes you to it. This does not work everywhere: the built-in browser of some systems does not support such notifications, and then the switch changes nothing.

## The hourglass is not a notification

An hourglass sometimes appears next to the bell: these are your own actions still waiting for the network’s confirmation — see [Confirming actions](transactions.md).
