# People and profiles

Profiles, subscriptions and blocking: how to find interesting people and keep unpleasant ones away.

## Where to find people

- **“Who to follow”** — in the right panel of the home screen when the window is wide enough. A guest sees the most popular authors there, and after signing in it shows people picked for you. Those you already follow are not in the list.
- **Search** — type `@name` in the search bar or open the **“Users”** tab in the results — see [Search](search.md).
- **The feed and comments** — click an author’s name or avatar to open their profile.
- **Subscriber and subscription lists** in any profile are another way to find people with similar interests.

## What you can do

- Look at a person’s profile — their posts, reputation, subscribers — see [Profile](profile.md).
- Subscribe so that their posts go to the **“Subscriptions”** section — see [Subscriptions](subscriptions.md).
- Write to them — **“Start chat”** in the profile or the chat button in the header of their post, see [Chats](chats.md).
- Keep away someone unpleasant — see [Blocking](blocking.md).

How to set up your own page — see [Your profile](edit-profile.md).
