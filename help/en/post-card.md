# A post in the feed

What a post in the feed consists of and what you can do with it: read it in full, view images, rate, comment, save.

## What it consists of

- **The header:** the author's avatar and name; the number next to the name is their [reputation](reputation.md), and the publishing time is under the name: click it to open the post in a window over the feed. Next to them are buttons to start a chat with the author, follow them and send a tip (your own posts have none), and on the right a bookmark for [favorites](favorites.md).
- **The text.** Long text is collapsed — the **“Show full”** button opens the whole post in a window over the feed. For an article it is **“Read article”** instead. Links in the text are coloured and underlined.
- **Images, video or audio.** Video and audio play right in the feed — see [Watching videos](video-player.md).
- **A link card** — the site the post links to: a square image on the left, the site name, title and description on the right. Click the image to see it in full in the gallery, and anywhere else on the card to open the site in a new browser window.
- **A poll**, if the author added one — see [Polls](polls.md).
- **A note instead of the post**, if the author opened it only to some readers — for example, subscribers or registered users — see [Visibility, language and publishing time](post-settings.md).
- **The category and tags.** Click a tag to filter the feed by it — see [Categories, tags and sorting](feed-filters.md).
- **The rating and action buttons** at the bottom.

**A repost** shows the original post inside, marked with whose it is. If the original post was deleted, it says **“Post deleted”** instead.

## Images

Click an image to open the gallery full screen. Page through with the arrows — on screen or on the keyboard; the mouse wheel zooms the image. The **“Download image”** button at the top left saves it to your device: on a computer the app asks where to save the file, on a phone the system “Share” sheet opens, and from there you can save the picture to the gallery or to files. Close the gallery with the Esc key or the cross.

## What you can do

- **Rate** with stars — see [Rating posts](ratings.md).
- **Comment** — see [Comments](comments.md).
- **“Repost”** — share the post on your page with a comment — see [Reposting and linking to a post](sharing.md).
- **“Share”** — a menu: **“Copy link”**, **“Copy embed code”**, **“Share via…”**, **“On blockchain”** — what the blockchain holds about the post, see [Block explorer](explorer.md#on-blockchain-without-leaving-the-feed) — **“Report”**, see [Reports](reports.md), and **“Block author”** (**“Unblock author”** for a blocked one), see [Blocking](blocking.md).
- **Thank the author** with coins — see [Tipping the author](tips.md).
- **“Promote”** — lift the post in the feed for PKOIN, your own or someone else’s — see [Promoting a post](boost.md).
- **Save** to favorites — the bookmark in the post's header.

Your own post also has **“Edit”** and **“Delete”** — see [Editing or deleting a post](edit-delete-post.md).

## The post page

Every post has its own page — with the whole text and all comments, and for a video also a **“More from this author”** block. From the post window, the **“Open as a page”** button next to the window title and the publishing time lead to it. It is also reached from `bastyon://` links to the post, from items in the Latest comments block and from clips in More from this author. The link that **“Copy link”** copies opens the post on the bastyon.com website — you can send it to anyone.

## A post that is not on the blockchain yet

You see your own freshly published post right away — in your profile feed, marked **“Not yet published to the blockchain”**. Until the network confirms it, it cannot be rated or commented on, and others do not see it yet. This usually takes about a minute — see [Confirming actions](transactions.md).
