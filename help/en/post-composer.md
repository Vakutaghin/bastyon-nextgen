# The post editor

Text, emoji, mentions, links and tags: everything in the new post window.

## Text

Write in the **“What's on your mind?”** field. The smiley button is **“Emoji”**; next to it in the desktop app is the [voice input](voice-input.md) button.

A post's length is limited: together with its service fields it has to fit into about 60,000 characters, an article into 120,000. That is a lot for an ordinary post; if the app says **“The post exceeds the allowed size”**, split the text into several posts or make it an [article](articles.md).

## Mentions

Type `@` and the first letters of a name — matching people appear under the field. Pick someone, and `@name` goes into the text. In the published post a mention becomes a link to the profile.

## Links

- **The first link** in the text gets a card with the page's title and image. If you do not want it, click **“Remove link preview”** on the card.
- **A video link** to YouTube, Vimeo or PeerTube turns into an embedded clip.
- **A post that is just a link** will not go through: a link needs at least 30 characters of your own text or an image — **“Add more text to the link”**. This protects against spam.

## Tags

A post cannot be published without tags: you need at least one, and you can add up to five. Start typing a tag under the text — popular tags appear; pick with the arrow keys or the mouse. Enter, a comma or a space adds the tag; you do not need to type the hash sign. Only letters and digits stay in a tag, and capitals become lowercase. Backspace in the empty field removes the last tag, the cross removes any.

Tags help people find the post: categories and feed filters work by them — see [Categories, tags and sorting](feed-filters.md).

## Draft

The app saves the text of an unfinished post by itself — on this device and separately for each account. Closed the window — the text will be there the next time you open it. Images and tags are not kept in the draft. After publishing, the draft is cleared.

## Publishing

Click **“Publish”**. If something is missing, the line to the left of the button tells you what:

- **“A post cannot be empty”**;
- **“Add at least one tag”** or **“Too many tags (maximum 5)”**;
- **“Add more text to the link”**;
- **“Add a title for the video”** — video and audio need a title;
- **“Add a poll question”** or **“A poll needs at least 2 options”**.

If the post was rejected by the network itself, for example because of the daily limit, the app names the reason — see [The network rejected an action](action-rejected.md).
