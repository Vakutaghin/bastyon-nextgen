# Profile

What a person’s page has: posts, reputation, subscribers, badges — and what you can do from there.

A profile opens when you click a person’s name or avatar — in the feed, in comments, in search, in subscriber lists.

## What is on the page

- **The cover** at the top. It can only be set in the previous Bastyon app. Without a cover, the page starts right with the avatar and name.
- **Avatar and name.** Under the name there are coloured badges **“Verified”**, **“Moderator”**, **“Shark”** and **“Established”**. Tap a badge to read what it means; more in [Account badges](account-badges.md).
- **“Reputation”**, **“Subscribers”** and **“Subscriptions”**. Click the number of subscribers or subscriptions to open the list — see [Subscriptions](subscriptions.md). What reputation is — see [Reputation](reputation.md).
- **“Information”** — what the person wrote about themselves, if anything.
- **The account address.** Click it to copy: **“Address copied”**. Below it is **“On blockchain”**: what the blockchain holds about the account — registration, balance, reputation, earnings, recent transactions, see [Block explorer](explorer.md#on-blockchain-without-leaving-the-feed).
- **A website**, if there is one, **“Publications:”** — how many posts the person has, and **“Registered:”** — when they registered.
- **The feed of this person’s posts**, newest first.

## Marks on the avatar

In the top left corner of an avatar — in the feed, in comments, in search, in subscriber lists, in notifications and in chats — there may be a mark: a blue check is “Verified”, a gold crown is “Moderator”, a purple flag is “Shark”. There is one mark: if several apply, the senior one shows. Point at a mark to see its name. What the badges mean and what they are given for — see [Account badges](account-badges.md).

## What you can do

- **“Subscribe”** — and the bell next to it to get notifications about their new posts — see [Subscriptions](subscriptions.md).
- **“Read in Messenger”** — the person’s posts appear in the messenger as a separate channel, next to your chats. You don’t need to subscribe for that, see [Channels](channels.md).
- **“Start chat”** — see [Chats](chats.md).
- **“Block”** — see [Blocking](blocking.md).

On a phone there is no side column: the name, **“Subscribe”**, **“Read in Messenger”** and the chat button are at the top of the page, above the posts.

Subscribing, reading in the messenger and blocking are available only when you are signed in. You can tip the person from the header of any of their posts — see [Tipping the author](tips.md).

On your own page these buttons are replaced by **“Edit profile”**, see [Your profile](edit-profile.md), and above the feed there is **“Create post”**.

## If a profile does not open

- **“Registration in progress”** — this is your profile, and the network has not confirmed the registration yet. It usually takes a few minutes — see [Signing up](create-account.md).
- **“User not found”** — there is no account with this name or address. Maybe the name has a typo, or the person changed their name: find them via [search](search.md).
- **“Account deleted”** instead of the name — the owner deleted the account in the previous Bastyon app.

The profile of someone you have blocked opens as usual, and their posts are visible on it: blocking only removes them from feeds.
