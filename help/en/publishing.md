# Publishing

How to write a post, an article or a poll, add images and videos, and what happens after you click Publish.

## Where to create a post

Click **“Create post”** above the feed. On a computer the editor opens in a window over the feed; on a phone it opens as a separate full-screen page. To publish, you need to be [signed in](sign-in.md), and a new account has to wait until the network confirms its [registration](create-account.md).

The **“Repost”** button under someone else's post opens the same editor with the original post inside — see [Reposting and linking to a post](sharing.md).

## What you can publish

- **A post** — text with images, links and tags, see [The post editor](post-composer.md) and [Images in a post](post-images.md).
- **A video or audio** from your device — see [Uploading video and audio](video-upload.md).
- **An article** — a long text with headings, images and quotes, see [Articles](articles.md).
- **A poll** — a question with answer options, see [Polls](polls.md).

In the post settings you can choose who can see the post, which language it is in and when it goes out — see [Visibility, language and publishing time](post-settings.md). In the desktop app you can dictate the text — see [Voice input](voice-input.md).

## After publishing

The post goes to the blockchain. For the first minute or two only you see it — in your profile feed, marked **“Not yet published to the blockchain”**. Once the network confirms it, the post appears in other people's feeds. More in [Confirming actions](transactions.md).

How many posts you can publish per day depends on the account status: 5 for a new account, 30 for a full one. See [Account status and limits](limits.md). To fix or remove a published post — [Editing or deleting a post](edit-delete-post.md).
