# Sign-up does not finish

The name is taken, the captcha fails, the sign-up coins do not arrive, the check drags on — what to do.

How sign-up works is in [Signing up](create-account.md). The main rule for every case below: **do not start signing up again with new keys**. The app keeps the sign-up you started and continues it with the same keys and coins — via **“Finish registration”** in the account menu.

## The name is taken or too long

The app checks whether a name is free while you type it. If the network still rejected it — say, someone took the name in the meantime — the app says **“This name is already taken.”** or **“This name is too long.”**. Choose **“Finish registration”** in the account menu and enter another name: the keys and coins stay the same.

## Captcha

- **“Too many attempts. Please refresh the captcha.”** — load a new picture and enter it.
- **“Too many attempts. Please try again later.”** — wait a little and continue from the same point.

## The coins do not arrive

The sign-up coins are handed out by the network’s proxies, and in limited amounts: no more than five accounts per IP address, and the next one no sooner than 10 days after the previous one. If the limit is used up, the app says: “Several accounts were registered from your IP address recently…”.

What you can do:

- try again in a few days;
- sign up from another network — for example, mobile data instead of home Wi-Fi;
- ask support for the coins — they will be sent to your address by hand; how to write is in [Reporting a bug](report-bug.md).

New keys from the same IP address will not get coins, so do not start over.

## The check drags on

After sending, the sign-up waits for the network to confirm it — usually about a minute. If there is no confirmation within 30 minutes, the app warns you. Check your internet and choose **“Finish registration”** in the account menu. If you restart the app, it also keeps waiting for the same sign-up.

Until the sign-up is confirmed, the network does not accept your actions: **“Your account is not activated yet. Wait until the network confirms your registration.”**
