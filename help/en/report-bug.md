# Reporting a bug

How to tell the developers about a problem and what to include so it can be found quickly.

## Where the button is

**“Report a bug”** is an item of the account menu: click your avatar in the header, and on a phone open the **“Account”** section of the side menu. It opens a [chat](messenger.md) with the Bastyon NextGen support account. Describe the problem there, like a regular message; you can attach a screenshot. The conversation is encrypted, like any private chat.

If you report often, put the bug icon in the header: Settings → [“Header and panels”](settings.md#header-and-panels).

The button works when you are signed in and the messenger is on.

## App details

In the chat with support, the **“App details”** button is pinned above the conversation. It collects what helps to find a bug:

- the Bastyon version and where it runs — computer, phone or browser;
- the system and its version, phone model, screen, language and time zone;
- theme, scale, and whether the messenger and Tor are on;
- whether there is internet, which node and chat server the app is connected to;
- recent app errors.

Before sending, a window shows everything that will go, word for word — you decide whether to send it. Keys, the recovery phrase, password, messages, contacts and browsing history are not part of it, and addresses, IDs and file paths are removed from error texts. The message is encrypted like everything in a private chat.

In the chat the details look like a small scrollable box with a copy button. If they do not fit into one message, they go in several parts, in order.

## What to write

- what you did, step by step;
- what you expected to see and what actually happened, with the exact error text;
- the app details — with the button in the chat with support; without the chat, **“App version”**, **“Build”** and **“Platform”** from **“Settings”** → **“Diagnostics”**;
- a screenshot, if the error is visible.

The more precise the steps, the faster the bug is found and fixed.

> [!CAUTION]
> Never send your recovery phrase or private key to anyone — not to support, not to the developers. They are not needed to look into a bug, and whoever gets them takes over the account and the coins.

## Without an account or without the chat

If you cannot sign in or the chat is turned off, describe the problem on GitHub, in the Issues section of the app’s repository: [github.com/Vakutaghin/bastyon-nextgen/issues](https://github.com/Vakutaghin/bastyon-nextgen/issues). This needs a GitHub account. Everything you write there is public.
