# Reports

When and how to report a post or a comment, and what happens next.

A report is for what the network’s rules directly forbid: erotica and pornography, child exploitation, threats of violence, drugs and spam. If a post is simply unpleasant or you disagree with it, give it a low rating or [block](blocking.md) the author — see [Moderation and rules](moderation.md).

## How to report

1. Open the menu:
   - on a post — **“Share”**, and at the bottom of the menu **“Report”**;
   - on a comment — **“Actions”** with three dots, and in it **“Report”**.
2. In the **“Report post”** or **“Report comment”** window choose a reason: **“Erotic / Porn”**, **“Child exploitation”**, **“Direct threat of violence”**, **“Illegal narcotics”** or **“Spam”**.
3. Click **“Submit report”**. Once the report goes to the network, **“Report submitted”** appears.

The item is there only when you are signed in and only on other people’s posts and comments.

## Who can report

Only an experienced account can report: one that is at least a year old and whose posts and comments have been rated by at least 100 different people, at least 25 of them for comments. This way new or bought accounts cannot flood someone with reports. For everyone else the network replies **“Your reputation does not allow reports yet.”**

Other restrictions:

- Up to 30 reports a day, after that — **“You have reached your report limit for the last 24 hours.”**
- One report per author in 30 days, even if the posts are different. To a repeated one the network replies **“You have already reported this.”**

## What happens next

A report is a public transaction: the blockchain shows who reported what and for which reason. It cannot be taken back.

A single report removes nothing. When an author collects enough reports within 30 days, the network assembles a jury of moderators, and it decides whether the rules were broken. If so, the author is banned, and nodes stop showing their posts in feeds. More in [Moderation and rules](moderation.md).
