# Reputation

What reputation is, how it goes up and down, and what it unlocks.

[Reputation](glossary.md#reputation) is a number the network calculates from the ratings of your posts and comments. It shows how interesting the community finds what you write, and it unlocks things that new accounts cannot do. You can see your reputation in your profile and on the **“Limits”** page in the account menu.

## Where it comes from

Reputation is changed by the ratings of experienced members:

| Rating | Author's reputation |
|---|---|
| 5 stars on a post | +2 |
| 4 stars on a post | +1 |
| 3 stars on a post | 0 |
| 2 stars on a post | −0.1 |
| 1 star on a post | −0.2 |
| like on a comment | +0.1 |

A dislike on a comment does not affect reputation, but it moves the comment down in the discussion.

<details>
<summary>Whose ratings count and which do not</summary>

The rules are written into the node code, and anyone can check them:

- Reputation is changed only by the ratings of experienced accounts: at least a year old, with posts and comments rated by at least 100 different people, at least 25 of them for comments. Ratings from other accounts still count towards the post's score but do not move the author's reputation.
- From one person to one author, no more than two post ratings count per two days. That way mutual rating does not inflate reputation.
- Ratings given more than 30 days after the post was published do not affect reputation.

</details>

## What it unlocks

- **A full account.** At a reputation of 100 the account stops being a [trial](glossary.md#trial-account) one, and the limits on posts, ratings and comments grow. A balance of 50 PKOIN does the same — see [Account status and limits](limits.md).
- **Low ratings.** You can give 1–3 stars from a reputation of 100: that way new accounts cannot mass-downrate other people's posts.
- **A badge in the profile.** From a reputation of 100 the profile shows the **“Established”** badge.

## When reputation gets in the way

- At a reputation of −12 or lower the app does not let you rate posts: **“Your reputation is too low to vote.”**
- Below −50 you cannot comment or rate comments.
- A comment whose author has a negative reputation and that has collected 5 or more dislikes is collapsed: **“Hidden due to low author reputation”**. The **“Show anyway”** button opens it.

## How not to lose it

Reputation drops with low ratings from experienced members. The best way to raise it is to write what others find interesting and to take part in discussions: likes on comments count towards reputation too. Rating in exchange works poorly — the network cuts it off.
