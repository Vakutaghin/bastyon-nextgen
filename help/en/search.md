# Search

How to find a person, a post or a tag — and also a block, a transaction or an address on the blockchain.

## The search box

The search box is in the header; on a phone it is at the top of the page that **“Search”** in the bottom bar opens. Start typing: from the second letter on, suggestions appear under the box by section — people, posts, tags and mini apps. Click a suggestion to open it, or **“All →”** next to a section to see all results.

A few tricks:

- **Enter** opens the results page.
- **`@name`** — go straight to the profile of the person with that name.
- **`#tag`** — show a feed of posts with this tag. You can give several tags separated by spaces.
- **A Bastyon link** — paste a link to a post or a profile, and the app opens it.

## Results

The results page has the **“Users”**, **“Posts”** and **“Tags”** tabs. The tabs appear when something is found in more than one section. At the bottom of the list there is **“Show more”** if there are more results.

Search covers the whole network, in any language. If nothing is found, try another word.

## Blockchain

If you type a block number, a block hash, a transaction ID or a wallet address, the suggestions get a **“Block explorer”** section: **“Open block”**, **“Open transaction”** or **“Open address”**. More in [Block explorer](explorer.md).

## History

When you click the empty search box, **“Recent”** appears under it — your last 10 queries and opened profiles. The cross next to a query is **“Remove from history”**; **“Clear”** erases the whole history.

The history is stored only on this device, separately for each account, and is removed when you sign out of the account.
