# Settings

Every settings tab in order: what each one holds and where to read more.

Settings open from the account menu — **“Settings”** — or, on a phone, from the side menu. They are available only once you are signed in. The language, theme and other app options are shared by all accounts on the device.

## General

- **“Language”** and **“Theme”** — see [Language and appearance](appearance.md).
- **“Content”**:
  - **“Show embedded videos”** — YouTube and Vimeo players right in the post; turned off, a link remains;
  - **“Autoplay videos”** — a video starts once it appears on screen;
  - **“Link previews”** — a card with the title and image under a link;
  - **“Comments order”** — the order a discussion opens in, see [Comments](comments.md);
  - **“Messenger”** — turns the chat off entirely, see [Messenger](messenger.md).
- **“IPFS module”** — in the desktop app, when the module is installed, see [The IPFS module](ipfs-module.md).
- Voice input — in the desktop app, see [Voice input](voice-input.md).
