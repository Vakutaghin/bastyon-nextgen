# Reposting and linking to a post

How to share a post in your own feed, send it to a chat, copy its link or embed it on a website.

## Reposting

1. Under the post, click **“Repost”**.
2. The **“Repost”** window opens with the original post. If you like, write your own text for it in the **“Add a comment (optional)…”** field.
3. Click **“Repost”**.

A repost is a new post of yours: it appears in your profile and in your subscribers’ feeds, with the original post inside it, showing the author’s name and avatar. Like any post, a repost has [visibility, language and publishing time](post-settings.md), and it counts towards the daily post limit — see [Account status and limits](limits.md). Images, video, a poll and tags cannot be added to a repost.

If the author deletes the original post, the repost shows **“Post deleted”** in its place, and such a repost can no longer be reposted.

## Linking to a post

The **“Share”** button under a post opens a menu:

- **“Send to chat”** — straight into your Bastyon chat, see below.
- **“Copy link”** — an address like `https://bastyon.com/post?s=…`. You can send it to anyone: the post opens on the bastyon.com website in any browser, without the app and without an account.
- **“Share via…”** — your device’s system Share menu. The item is there only where the system supports it.
- **Telegram**, **X / Twitter**, **Facebook**, **Reddit**, **WhatsApp** — open the posting page of that network with a link to the post.

The menu also has **“Copy embed code”**, **“On blockchain”** and **“Report”** — about them below and in [Blockchain explorer](explorer.md#on-blockchain-without-leaving-the-feed) and [Reports](reports.md).

In a Bastyon chat a link to a post is shown as a card: the author, an image and the beginning of the text. Click the card to open the post — see [Messages](messages.md).

If a post is visible only to subscribers or only to signed-in users, whoever gets the link has to meet that condition, otherwise they see a note instead of the post — see [Visibility, language and publishing time](post-settings.md).

## Sending to a chat

A post, a comment, a profile, a collection and more can go straight into one of your Bastyon chats, without copying a link. The **“Send to chat”** item is:

- in the **“Share”** menu under a post and in a comment’s menu;
- in the **“Share profile”** menu on a person’s page — on your own too, to send your profile to a friend;
- on a collection or playlist page and in a row’s menu in the **“Collections”** section;
- in the menu of a video or track in **“Video”** and **“Audio”**, in playlists and in **“Media studio”**;
- on a file and on a permanent link in **“My files”**;
- on a transaction, block and address in the blockchain explorer — the **“To chat”** button.

The **“Send to chat”** window opens: at the top what you are sending, below your chats, the freshest first, with a search by name. Tick one or several chats, up to ten at a time, add a caption if you like and click **“Send”**. Each chat gets one message: the caption and the link. The window closes at once, and the message joins the chat just like one typed there: with no connection to the chat server it waits and goes out when the connection is back, and one that failed can be retried in the chat itself.

In the chat a link to a post, video or comment becomes a post card, a link to a profile a card with the avatar, nickname and reputation, a link to a collection or playlist a card with the cover, title, number of items and author, and a link to an IPFS file a file card. Clicking a card opens it in the app. A link to a mini app opens in the app too, and for someone using the old client all of them lead to bastyon.com. The app builds a profile link from the address, not the nickname: a nickname can change, and a link in a conversation should stay right.

A link to a private file can’t go to a public group or a group of 50 members or more: messages there aren’t encrypted, and the server would see the file key. Such chats are unavailable in the window and marked **“Not encrypted — not allowed here”**.

At the bottom of the window there is **“Copy link”** and, where the system supports it, **“Share via…”** — for when the link has to go somewhere other than a Bastyon chat. Only signed-in users have Bastyon chats: without signing in there is no **“Send to chat”** item. Channels and mesh chats are not listed.

## Linking to a comment

In a comment’s menu — the **“Actions”** button with three dots — choose **“Send to chat”** or **“Share”**. Where there is a system Share menu, it opens; otherwise the link is copied: **“Comment link copied”**. Such a link opens the post scrolled to that comment.

## Embedding a post on a website

**“Copy embed code”** in the **“Share”** menu copies a piece of HTML — **“Embed code copied”**. Paste it into your page’s code: the page will show the post through a widget from bastyon.com.
