# Subscriptions

How to subscribe to someone, get notifications about their posts and see who is subscribed to you.

## Subscribing and unsubscribing

You can subscribe:

- in the person’s profile — the **“Subscribe”** button;
- in the header of their post — the button with a person icon next to the name;
- in **“Who to follow”** on the home screen and in subscriber and subscription lists.

The button changes to **“Subscribed”**. Click it again to unsubscribe: **“Unsubscribed”**.

Posts of the people you follow are collected in the **“Subscriptions”** section of the left panel, newest first — see [Feed](feed.md).

## Notifications about new posts

Next to the subscribe button in a profile there is a bell, **“Enable notifications”**. With it you get notifications about this person’s new posts — see [Notifications](notifications.md). If you are not subscribed yet, the bell subscribes you as well.

**“Disable notifications”** is the same bell once more: the subscription stays, the notifications stop. If you click **“Subscribed”**, both the subscription and the notifications go.

## Who follows whom

Every profile shows the numbers of **“Subscribers”** and **“Subscriptions”**. Click a number to open a list with names and reputation. You can subscribe to someone right from the list, and **“Show more”** at the bottom loads more people.

A subscription is a blockchain record. Who follows whom and who has notifications on is visible to everyone — see [Privacy: what others can see](privacy.md).

## When you cannot subscribe

- Without signing in: **“Sign in to subscribe”**.
- If you have blocked the person or they have blocked you, the network does not accept the subscription — see [Blocking](blocking.md).
