# Tipping the author

How to send the author PKOIN as a thank-you for a post or a comment, and what it costs.

## How to send

1. Click **“Tip”**. On a post it is the gift button in the header, next to the subscribe button. On a comment it is an item in the **“Actions”** menu with three dots.
2. In the **“Tip the author”** window choose an amount — 1, 5, 10 or 50 — or type your own in the **“Amount in PKOIN”** field. Below the field you see how many coins you have available.
3. Click **“Send tip”**. Once the transfer goes to the network, **“Tip sent”** appears.

The button is there only when you are signed in and only on other people’s posts and comments. The tip for a post goes to whoever published it: for a repost, to the author of the repost.

## What it costs

The network fee is 0.00000001 PKOIN, and it is taken from the amount: the author receives that tiny bit less. Nothing else is charged.

The app will not send the transfer if:

- **“Amount is too small”** — the amount is not larger than the fee;
- **“Insufficient funds”** — your address has fewer coins than you entered;
- **“Enter a valid amount”** — the field is not a number or is not above zero.

Where to get coins — see [How to get PKOIN](how-to-buy-pkoin.md).

## What the author sees

The author gets a **“Tip received”** notification with the amount — if it is at least 0.05 PKOIN: the app does not show tiny transfers, so as not to let spam through. A tip differs from an ordinary transfer by a mark in the transaction itself, which the previous Bastyon app understands too. In the author’s wallet the tip is an incoming transfer from you. A tip is not tied to a post: the author sees who sent how much, but not what for. If you want them to know, write to them — in a comment, for example.

Like any transfer, a tip is recorded on the blockchain, and anyone can see who sent how much to whom.

<details>
<summary>Coins with a comment in the previous app</summary>

In the previous Bastyon app coins could be attached to a comment: the post’s author received them together with the comment, and the comment itself was lifted in the discussion. This app cannot send them that way, but it takes such coins into account: comments with them rank higher in the “Most interesting first” order.
