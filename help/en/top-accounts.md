# Top accounts

The accounts with the highest reputation in the network, and the richest in PKOIN among them.

## Where to find it

**“Top accounts”** is in the left panel, under **“Services”**; on a phone it is in the side menu. You can view the top without signing in.

## Order

At the top there is a switch:

- **“By reputation”** — from the highest [reputation](reputation.md) down;
- **“By PKOIN”** — from the largest balance down.

The chosen order is in the page address, so you can share a link to the top.

Each account shows its place, the avatar with [badges](account-badges.md), the name, the year it joined, subscribers and posts, and on the right the reputation and the PKOIN balance. On a phone there is one number on the right — the one the list is sorted by; the other is in the line under the name. Your account, if it is in the top, is highlighted and marked **“You”**. Click a row to open the profile.

The page starts with 30 accounts, and the next ones appear when you scroll to the end. Names, avatars and balances load as you scroll — until they arrive, grey bars stand in their place.

## Where the list comes from

The Pocketnet network has no list of all accounts by reputation or by balance: the nodes do not give one out. So the top is built like this:

- it includes the 300 subscribers with the highest reputation of the network’s most popular authors, and those authors themselves. Almost the whole active part of the network follows them, so by reputation it is nearly the full top. Someone who follows none of them will not get in;
- a node gives out the balance one address at a time, not as a list. So **“By PKOIN”** is the richest of the same 300 accounts with the highest reputation. The first time you open this order, the app gets the balances of all 300 — the top of the page shows “Getting balances: 120 of 300” — and only then shows the places. A large PKOIN holder with low reputation will not get in.

If a node did not answer about someone’s balance, a dash stands instead of the amount, and the account goes to the end of the **“By PKOIN”** list.

The first time, the top takes up to half a minute to build: the nodes sort hundreds of thousands of subscribers. After that it is kept in memory for half an hour; restart the app to build it again.
