# Top accounts

The accounts with the highest reputation in the network.

## Where to find it

**“Top accounts”** is in the left panel, under **“Services”**; on a phone it is in the side menu. You can view the top without signing in.

## What the list shows

Accounts go from the highest [reputation](reputation.md) down. Each one shows its place, the avatar with [badges](account-badges.md), the name, the year it joined, subscribers and posts, and the reputation on the right. Your account, if it is in the top, is highlighted and marked **“You”**. Click a row to open the profile.

The page starts with 30 accounts, and the next ones appear when you scroll to the end. Names and avatars load as you scroll — until they arrive, grey bars stand in their place.

## Where the list comes from

The Pocketnet network has no list of all accounts by reputation: the nodes do not give one out. So the top includes the 300 subscribers with the highest reputation of the network’s most popular authors, and those authors themselves. Almost the whole active part of the network follows them, so it is nearly the full top. Someone who follows none of them will not get in.

## Updates

The top builds itself when you open the page — there is nothing to update by hand. The first time it takes up to half a minute: the nodes sort hundreds of thousands of subscribers. After that it is kept in memory for half an hour, and then it is built again the next time you open the page. While it builds, the previous list stays on screen.
