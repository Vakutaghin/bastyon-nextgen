# Troubleshooting

What to do when something does not work: the connection, actions on the network, sign-up, images and videos.

## First

1. Check that you have internet: open any website.
2. If [Tor](tor.md) is on, try turning it off: it is often the cause.
3. Restart the app.
4. Check for a new version — the bug may already be fixed, see [Updates](updates.md).
5. Look at **“Settings”** → **“Diagnostics”**: it shows whether there is a connection and which node the app is connected to — see [Network and nodes](network.md).

## What happened

- The feed does not load, the chat does not connect — [No connection to the network](connection-problems.md).
- A post is not published, a rating is not set, a transfer does not go through — [The network rejected an action](action-rejected.md).
- Sign-up does not finish — [Sign-up does not finish](registration-problems.md).
- No images, a video does not play — [Images or videos do not show](media-problems.md).
- You lost access to your account — [If you lost access](lost-access.md).

## Still stuck

Write to the developers — see [Reporting a bug](report-bug.md).
