# Using this help

Contents, index, search and favorites, help on F1 and “?” buttons, links to articles.

Help opens from the left panel or the account menu — **“Help”**. It is built into the app: it describes exactly the installed version and works without the internet.

## The navigator

On the left there are four tabs:

- **“Contents”** — all articles, by book;
- **“Index”** — words and terms in alphabetical order: start typing in the **“Word or term”** field;
- **“Search”** — through the text of every article, word forms included. In the open article the words found are highlighted — **“Clear highlighting”** removes it;
- **“Favorites”** — articles you starred.

Buttons above the article: **“Back”** and **“Forward”** — through the articles you read, **“Help home”**, **“Hide panel”**, the star — **“Add to favorites”**, and **“Copy link”**. From the desktop app and the phone, the link leads to this article in the app’s open repository — anyone can open it, even without Bastyon.

## Help in place

F1 on any screen opens the article about that screen at the side, while the screen itself stays where it is. The **“Open in help”** button expands the article into the full help section. If there is no article about the screen, the help home opens. See [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md).

## An article

- If an article is about something not every device has, a line at the top says “Works in: …”.
- Underlined terms show an explanation when you hover or tap them, and **“More in the glossary”** leads to the [Glossary](glossary.md).
- **“Tip”**, **“Important”** and **“Warning”** boxes highlight the key points.
- At the bottom are **“Previous”** and **“Next”**: this way the help can be read straight through, like a book.

## Language

The help exists in Russian and English. With any other interface language it opens in English. If an article is not translated yet, you see the Russian text with a note about it.
