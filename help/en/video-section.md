# The Video section

Where to watch Bastyon videos: new and best, subscriptions, playlists, saved videos and search.

The section opens with **“Video”** in the left panel, on a phone — in the side menu. Videos come as a grid: preview, length, title, author, number of views and date. Tap a video to open its post in a window: the video starts right away, with the text, ratings and comments below. **“Open as a page”** in the window title opens the post as a page, with an address you can share. About the player — see [Watching videos](video-player.md).

## Grid or posts

The buttons on the right above the feed switch the view: **“Grid”** shows videos as tiles, **“Posts”** shows whole posts, as in the main feed, with the player, text and comments. The view is remembered on this device, separately for the Video and Audio sections.

## Tabs

- **“Home”** — videos from the whole network in the feed language. **“New”** shows recent ones, **“Best of the week”** the best rated over the last seven days.
- **“Subscriptions”** — videos by the authors you follow.
- **“Playlists”** — your public playlists and private playlists — see [Collections and playlists](collections.md) and [Private lists](media-library.md).
- **“Saved”** and **“Watch later”** — your private lists.
- **“Uploads”** — your videos in the [Media studio](media-studio.md).

Everything except Home is available after you sign in. Videos by authors you have blocked are not shown.

## Search

The search field next to the title looks for videos by the post's title and description across the whole network. Results replace the tab; clear the field to go back.

## The video menu

The three-dot button on a video:

- **“Watch later”** and **“Save”** — to your private lists; press again to remove;
- **“Play next”** — queue it right after what is playing now;
- **“Add to…”** — to a private or public playlist — see [Collections and playlists](collections.md).

## A playlist in a row

Open a playlist or a private list and press **“Play all”** or **“Shuffle”** — the videos play one after another. An **“Up next”** panel with the list appears under the video: tap any to jump to it. When a video ends, the next starts in five seconds; you can cancel the countdown. The panel's buttons are the previous and next video, shuffle, repeat and closing the queue.
