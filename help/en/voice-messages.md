# Voice messages

How to record and send a voice message, and why the record button does not work on some computers.

## Recording

The microphone button to the right of the input field is **“Record voice message”**. It is there while the field is empty.

It works the same with a mouse and with a finger:

- press and hold the button while you speak. Let go, and the message is sent;
- slide up to the lock above the button to lock the recording, so you no longer have to hold. Then **“Cancel”** and a send button appear at the bottom;
- slide left to cancel the recording. The Esc key cancels it too;
- if you press and let go at once, nothing is recorded: a hint “Hold to record, release to send” appears above the button.

With the keyboard, Enter or Space on the button starts a recording that is already locked.

Recording responds from the first touch: the time appears at once, and the dot next to it starts blinking when the microphone is actually recording — usually almost at once.

## Listening

A voice message has a **“Play”** button and a progress bar: tap the bar to jump to a point. Voice messages are encrypted the same way as files — see [Photos, videos and files in chat](chat-attachments.md).

## If recording does not start

The app says what is in the way:

- **“No access to the microphone. Allow it for the app in the system settings: Privacy → Microphone.”** — you or the system denied access. Allow it and try again;
- **“No access to the microphone. Allow it for the app in the phone settings.”** — on Android. Usually the phone asks for permission at the first recording. After “Allow”, press and hold the button again: recording goes on only while the button is held. If you denied it for good, it no longer asks: tap the message to open the app’s page in the phone settings and turn the microphone on there;
- **“No microphone found.”** or **“The microphone is busy with another app.”**;
- **“Voice messages cannot be recorded here: the built-in browser gives no access to the microphone.”** — the app’s built-in browser on this system does not give the page a microphone. Record the voice message on a phone or in the previous Bastyon app.

Do not confuse voice messages with [voice input](voice-input.md): that turns speech into text, and the recording is not sent anywhere.
