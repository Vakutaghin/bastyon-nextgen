# Voice messages

How to record and send a voice message, and why the record button does not work on some computers.

## Recording

The microphone button to the right of the input field is **“Record voice message”**. It is there while the field is empty.

- **With a mouse:** press and hold the button while you speak. Let go, and the message is sent.
- **With a finger:** hold the button while you speak and lift your finger to send. Slide left to cancel the recording, slide up to lock it so you no longer have to hold. Then **“Cancel”** and a send button appear at the bottom.

While recording, you see how long it has been going.

## Listening

A voice message has a **“Play”** button and a progress bar: tap the bar to jump to a point. Voice messages are encrypted the same way as files — see [Photos, videos and files in chat](chat-attachments.md).

## If recording does not start

The app says what is in the way:

- **“No access to the microphone. Allow it for the app in the system settings: Privacy → Microphone.”** — you or the system denied access. Allow it and try again;
- **“No microphone found.”** or **“The microphone is busy with another app.”**;
- **“Voice messages cannot be recorded here: the built-in browser gives no access to the microphone.”** — the app’s built-in browser on this system does not give the page a microphone. Record the voice message on a phone or in the previous Bastyon app.

Do not confuse voice messages with [voice input](voice-input.md): that turns speech into text, and the recording is not sent anywhere.
