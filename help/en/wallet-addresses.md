# Balances and addresses

The main and additional wallets, the total balance and how to receive PKOIN at your address.

## The “Balances” tab

At the top there are three numbers: **“Main wallet balance”**, **“Total balance across all additional wallet addresses”** and **“Total balance of all wallets”**. Below are tables of wallets with addresses and balances. The icon next to an address is **“Open in block explorer”**: all transactions of that address, see [Block explorer](explorer.md).

## The main wallet

**“Main wallet”** is your account’s address, the same as in your profile. Tips and transfers arrive at it, fees are paid from it, and you can send PKOIN from it.

## Additional wallets

**“Additional wallets”** are a few more addresses derived from the same recovery phrase. Their addresses start with a **Z**. The previous Bastyon app introduced them, and there you could receive and keep coins on them.

In this app they are **view-only**: you see the balance, but you cannot send coins from them — the app cannot sign such transfers yet. That is why it does not offer them for receiving either. If an additional wallet has coins, you can manage them in the previous app.

The pencil button is **“Rename wallet”**: a label that helps you tell the wallets apart. It is kept only on this device.

## Receiving PKOIN

1. Open the **“Transfers”** tab and choose **“Receive”**.
2. Click **“Show receive address”** — the main wallet’s address and its QR code appear.
3. **“Copy”** — the address goes to the clipboard, and you can send it to whoever is paying.

The address is not a secret: all anyone can do with it is send you coins. But never show anyone your recovery phrase or private key — see [Recovery phrase and private key](recovery-phrase.md).
