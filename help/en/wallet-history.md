# Transaction history

Where to see the PKOIN you sent and received, and how to find a transaction on the blockchain.

## The “History” tab

The wallet’s **“History”** tab shows **“Sent and received PKOIN for your main wallet.”**, newest first. Each entry has:

- **“Received”** or **“Sent”** and the amount;
- from or to whom — an address;
- a mark if it is not an ordinary transfer: **“Tip”**, **“Boost”** — promoting a post, **“Stake”** — a block reward;
- the time. Hover over it to see the exact date.

At the bottom there is **“Load more”** if there are many entries.

Fees for posts, ratings and other actions are not shown in the history: the coins do not go to other people then, but come back to you as change minus the tiny fee.

## Transaction details

Click an entry — it opens in the [block explorer](explorer.md): the block it is recorded in, the inputs and outputs, the fee and the message, if there is one.

## What is not here

- Transactions of additional wallets — you can see them in the block explorer via the button next to the address in the **“Balances”** tab, see [Balances and addresses](wallet-addresses.md).
- Transfers not yet confirmed by the network: they appear after confirmation.
