# Wallet and PKOIN

The wallet is built into your account: balances, transfers, history and earnings in one section.

## Where the wallet is

- On a computer the balance is shown in the header, under your name.
- The wallet section is **“Wallets”** in the account menu, on a phone **“Wallet”** in the bottom bar.

The wallet is there only when you are signed in.

## What is in it

- **“Balances”** — how many coins are on the main and additional wallets, see [Balances and addresses](wallet-addresses.md).
- **“Transfers”** — send PKOIN or receive it, see [Transfers and fees](send-pkoin.md).
- **“History”** — coins received and sent, see [Transaction history](wallet-history.md).
- **“Earnings”** — how much you have received over all time, see [Earnings](earnings.md).
- **“Buy/Sell”** — the PKOIN rate.

## The PKOIN rate

The **“Buy/Sell”** tab shows **“PKOIN Rate (USD)”**: a chart of the price in dollars over the chosen **“Period”**, from a day to a year, the current rate, the change over the period, the maximum and the minimum. The data comes from the CoinGecko website.

You cannot buy or sell PKOIN in the app itself. Where to do it — follow the “How to get PKOIN” link there: it opens the [How to get PKOIN](how-to-buy-pkoin.md) article at the side without leaving the wallet.

## The wallet is your key

Coins are stored neither in the app nor on a server but on the blockchain, at your account’s address. Only someone who has the key — that is, the [recovery phrase](recovery-phrase.md) — can spend them. Lose the phrase and you lose the coins: nobody can restore them. If someone else learns it, they can transfer them away.
